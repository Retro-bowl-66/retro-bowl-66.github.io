Inside 1010 Color Match: Block placing with a color-sorting twist

Potato Jam took the familiar block-fitting puzzle and gave it a second thing to worry about. Released in 2023, this version asks you to place colored plates on a large board and bring matching colors together so they clear away. Where the classic formula cares only about shape, here every placement has to make sense in two ways.

Gameplay overview

Pieces arrive a few at a time, and you drag each one onto any free spot on the grid. Plates of the same color that end up together are eliminated, opening space for whatever comes next. The session continues until a piece fits nowhere, so the real goal is keeping the board breathable while chasing points.

Beginner's checklist

  • Look at every waiting piece before placing the first one.
  • Set new plates beside existing ones of the same color whenever the shape allows.
  • Leave a few open gaps of different sizes so awkward pieces have somewhere to go.

Control scheme

  • Mouse – drag / Drop

Strategy notes

Think of the board in zones. Grouping colors loosely in different areas means most incoming plates have a natural home, which cuts down on panic placements. When a piece fits only in a bad spot, weigh the color match it creates against the space it blocks. Space usually wins.

Final verdict

The color layer adds real decisions to a formula that can feel mechanical, and dragging pieces is comfortable with a mouse. Sessions can drift once you find a rhythm, and the visuals are pleasant rather than distinctive. Good for puzzle players who like slow, low-pressure thinking.
