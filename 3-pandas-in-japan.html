3 Pandas in Japan Online

Inside 3 Pandas in Japan: Three pandas, one problem per screen

FlashTeam's 2018 puzzle sends a trio of pandas across a hand-drawn Japan, and nothing about the route is cooperative. Each screen is a small obstacle course that none of them could clear alone. You decide who moves, who jumps and who climbs onto whom, and the answer is rarely the first thing you try.

Gameplay overview

Everything happens through clicks, but where you click changes what it means. Click a spot on the path and a panda walks forward, click the air above and it jumps, click another panda and they stack. Objects in the scenery often react to clicks as well. The goal on every screen is to get all three animals through.

Beginner's checklist

  • Click around each new scene before moving any panda, to see what reacts.
  • Use stacking to reach high ledges or objects that one panda cannot touch.
  • Watch the background for clues, since the solution often hides in scenery.
  • If the pandas end up badly placed, restart the screen rather than forcing it.

Control scheme

  • Clicking on Road – move Forward
  • Clicking on Air – jump
  • Clicking on Panda – stack
  • Note – clicking has other functionalities depending on where you click.

Strategy notes

Treat each screen as a sequence puzzle. Usually one panda needs to do something first, such as triggering an object, before the others can follow. When stuck, work backward from the exit and ask what the last step must be. Trying odd clicks is often quicker than staring at the screen waiting for inspiration.

Final verdict

The art is charming, the puzzles are short and the sense of discovery is real. The downside is that some solutions feel arbitrary rather than logical, which can leave you clicking at random. A gentle pick for anyone who likes cartoon puzzles over twitchy action.
