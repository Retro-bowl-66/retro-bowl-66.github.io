About Retro-Bowl-66

Retro-Bowl-66 is a free browser game site with 450 games and a written guide for each one. You can search the library, browse four categories (Action, Strategy, Casual, and Driving & sports) and play straight away in your browser, with no account and nothing to install.

Why every game has a guide

Most game sites give you a thumbnail and a play button and leave the rest to luck. We think a short break deserves better odds. Each guide is written like a brief review: what the game is about, how to get started, how to improve and a fair verdict on its strengths and weak spots. Reading one takes a minute or two and can spare you ten minutes of a game that was never your type.

How games are chosen

We look for games that load reliably in a browser, have controls that make sense and offer something worth returning to, whether that is a tight puzzle, a satisfying driving model or a simple idea done well. We aim for variety across the four categories rather than dozens of copies of the same idea.

How games are removed

Games break, get abandoned or are withdrawn by their makers. When a game stops loading, changes so much that its guide no longer fits, or its owner asks us to take it down, we remove it or rewrite the guide.

How the site is funded

Retro-Bowl-66 is free because it shows ads, served through Google AdSense. The ads pay for hosting and for the time it takes to write guides. Advertisers have no influence over which games we list or what our verdicts say.

Who owns the games

The games are made and hosted by independent developers and publishers, and they remain their property. We write the guides and organize the library. If you made a game listed here and would like it updated, credited or removed, get in touch.
