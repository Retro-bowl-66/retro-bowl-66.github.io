Accessibility

We want Retro-Bowl-66 to work for as many people as possible, including those who navigate by keyboard, use a screen reader or rely on magnification. This page describes what we aim for and where the limits are.

Keyboard use

The menus, search box, category links and game buttons are built to be reached with the Tab key and activated with Enter. Once a game has loaded, click inside it or tab into its frame so it receives your key presses. If you open a game in fullscreen, the Escape key takes you back to the page.

Images and alt text

Game thumbnails carry alt text with the game's name, so screen reader users can tell games apart in the grid. Purely decorative images are kept out of the way of assistive technology.

Contrast and readability

The site uses a dark theme with light text, chosen for strong contrast. Guides use clear headings and short sections so they are easy to scan, and you can zoom the page in your browser without losing content.

Responsive layout

Pages adapt to phones, tablets and desktop screens. The game grid reflows to fit the width of your screen, and guides stay readable at narrow sizes.

Limits of third-party games

The games themselves are made by other developers, and we cannot change how they work. Many depend on fast reactions, precise mouse movement, color cues or sound, and few offer remappable controls or screen reader support. Each guide lists the controls and describes what the game asks of you, so you can judge whether it will suit you before you start.

Contact

If something on the site is hard to use, email contact@retro-bowl-66.github.io and tell us the page, the problem and the browser or assistive tool you use. We will try to fix it or suggest a workaround.
