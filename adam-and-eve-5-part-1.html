Adam and Eve 5 Part 1 Online

Inside Adam and Eve 5 Part 1: A point and click puzzle that jokes about itself

Functu Games has built a running gag into this series: the developers insist everything about it is bad, starting with the name. Released in December 2018, this first half of the fifth entry keeps the joke going while sending Adam on yet another search for Eve. The self-mockery is part of the charm, and it lowers expectations nicely.

Gameplay overview

Each scene is a small obstacle course between Adam and the way out. You click objects, creatures and bits of scenery to change them, clearing a route so Adam can keep walking. Solutions are often silly: something needs to be poked, distracted or knocked over in the right order. Once the path is clear, a new scene begins.

Beginner's checklist

  • Click everything on screen at least once before deciding on a solution.
  • Watch what each click changes, because order often matters.
  • Note any click that seems to do nothing; it may only work later in the sequence.

Control scheme

  • Click – action

Strategy notes

Most puzzles follow a cause and effect chain of a few steps. Identify the final obstacle in Adam's way first, then look for the object that can remove it, and then for whatever unlocks that object. Clickable things are not always obvious, so methodically trying each part of the scene beats waiting for an idea.

Final verdict

Short, goofy and easy to pick up, it suits a quick puzzle break. The humor will not land for everyone, and some solutions are random enough to feel like guessing. Fans of light cartoon puzzles will enjoy it, while players after deep logic should look elsewhere.
