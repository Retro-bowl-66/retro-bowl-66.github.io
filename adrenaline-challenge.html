Adrenaline Challenge Online

Inside Adrenaline Challenge: Collect the dots, keep the bike upright

Blackmaze's 2009 trials game has an unusually tidy brief for a motorcycle game. Every course is scattered with green dots, and your job is to gather all of them before riding to the exit. Speed helps, but balance is the real skill, because the terrain is all lumps, ramps and drops that seem designed to tip you over.

Gameplay overview

You control the throttle and lean of a dirt bike on a side-on course. Shifting your weight keeps the wheels where you want them on climbs and landings, while braking helps on steep descents. Missing a dot means turning back for it, so the route matters as much as the riding. A bad landing ends the attempt quickly.

Beginner's checklist

  • Ride slowly through a new course first to spot where every dot sits.
  • Lean back on climbs and forward on landings to keep both wheels down.
  • Plan an order for collecting dots so you avoid doubling back.
  • Ease off the throttle before jumps instead of hitting them flat out.

Strategy notes

Most crashes come from landing at an angle. In the air, adjust your lean so the bike meets the ground with both wheels together or the rear wheel slightly first. Grab awkward dots while the course is fresh in your mind, and save easy ones for the route to the exit. A slower clean run beats a fast run that ends in the dirt.

Final verdict

The dot-collecting goal gives each course a clear shape, and the physics are readable once you adjust. It shows its age in presentation, and some layouts feel more fiddly than clever. A solid choice for players who enjoy careful, balance-based riding.
