Adventure Drivers Online

Inside Adventure Drivers: Side-on racing where items matter as much as speed

Domas Kazragis built this 2018 racer around a simple idea: on a bumpy track, the car that uses its tools best usually wins. You race rivals across hilly side-view courses, tilting through the air, boosting on flats and firing off power ups at the right moment. It plays quickly, and races are short enough to repeat without much thought.

Gameplay overview

Holding up accelerates, left and right tilt the car, and separate keys handle jumping, boosting and triggering whatever item you are carrying. The tracks roll up and down constantly, so landing flat is the difference between keeping speed and bouncing to a crawl. Items collected along the way can help you or hinder rivals, and timing them is half the race.

Beginner's checklist

  • Tilt the car to match the slope of the ground before each landing.
  • Save boost for flat or uphill stretches where it helps most.
  • Use items soon after collecting them unless a better moment is close.
  • Jump over rough patches instead of bouncing through them.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – move
  • Left / Right Arrows – tilt
  • Space – jump
  • Shift – boost
  • Enter – power Up
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Think of each race in sections. On downhill stretches gravity does the work, so hold boost for the climbs that follow. Use jumps to skip bumpy ground and set up clean landings. With items, the best moment is often just before a rival overtakes or as you approach a tricky section, rather than the instant you pick one up.

Final verdict

Lively physics and the item system make races feel busy in a good way. Some results lean too heavily on luck, and the presentation is functional rather than memorable. A reliable pick for short racing sessions with a dash of chaos.
