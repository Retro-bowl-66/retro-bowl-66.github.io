Inside Amidst the Clouds: A platformer where the air does half the work

Casper van Dommelen's platformer replaces the usual springs and moving platforms with something softer: drifting white smoke and currents of air. Step into a cloud and you float upward or sink, catch a gust and you are carried sideways. Each level asks only that you reach the exit, but getting there means learning to ride the atmosphere.

Gameplay overview

Levels are compact puzzles built around these airflows. You move left and right and jump, while smoke and currents add vertical and sideways motion you cannot fully control. The challenge is choosing when to enter a current, when to drop out of it and how to string several together. Mistimed exits leave you short of the next ledge.

Beginner's checklist

  • Watch how each kind of air current moves you before trusting it.
  • Enter currents from the edge so you have room to steer.
  • Look over the whole level first and trace a rough route to the exit.
  • Take small hops between currents rather than long uncontrolled leaps.

Strategy notes

Think of currents as paths rather than obstacles. The best routes usually chain one flow into the next with minimal walking, so spend a moment mapping which current connects to which. When a jump keeps failing, try leaving the current a little earlier. Gentle, short inputs work better than holding a direction down and hoping.

Final verdict

The airflow idea is fresh and gives levels a calm, floaty character. The physics can feel slippery, and some jumps take more retries than they should. A good pick for puzzle-minded platformer fans who prefer thinking over reflexes.
