Among Us Online

Inside Among Us: A fan-made solo shift as the impostor

Innersloth's 2018 hit is built for groups of friends lying to each other, which raises an obvious question about a single-player browser version. This fan-made take answers by casting you as the impostor and filling the ship with computer-controlled crewmates. It is not the original game, and the rights to Among Us stay with its creators, but it borrows the idea well enough.

Gameplay overview

Your job is to eliminate the crew without getting caught. Crewmates wander between rooms, so you look for moments when one is alone, strike and slip away before anyone notices. Being spotted at the wrong moment turns the round against you. Clear the ship and you win, which takes patience more than speed.

Beginner's checklist

  • Learn the map layout first so you know where crewmates tend to go.
  • Wait for crewmates to separate rather than striking near a group.
  • Move away from the scene quickly after each elimination.
  • Keep an eye on where other crewmates are heading before you act.

Strategy notes

Patience wins rounds. Follow a crewmate at a distance, wait until the room is empty of witnesses and only then act. Afterward, head somewhere ordinary instead of lingering. Computer crewmates are less cunning than human players, so the challenge is mostly about timing and positioning. Use the quiet edges of the map to set up your chances.

Final verdict

It captures the sneaky impostor fantasy in a form you can play alone, and rounds are short. Without real people to deceive, much of what makes Among Us special is missing. Fine for a curious few minutes, but fans of the original should know what they are getting.
