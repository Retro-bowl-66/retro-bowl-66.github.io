Inside Aquapark Slides: A waterslide race where the corners decide everything

Picture a waterpark designed by someone who has never heard of safety regulations, and you are close to the setting of GameApp Studio's 2019 racer. You and a pack of other riders launch from the top of an enormous twisting slide. The first to splash into the pool at the bottom wins, and everyone else gets wet anyway.

Gameplay overview

Steering is just left and right, but the slide does not make it easy. Curves throw you toward the walls, other riders bump into you and the track twists in ways that reward reading ahead. Your line through each bend affects how much speed you carry out of it, and one late mistake can cost a win.

Beginner's checklist

  • Watch the track ahead rather than your rider so you can prepare for turns.
  • Make small steering corrections instead of sharp swerves.
  • Stay clear of clusters of other riders, where bumps slow you down.

Strategy notes

Smooth lines are faster than aggressive ones. Drifting into the wall on a curve usually scrubs off speed, so begin each turn early and let the slide carry you. Other riders are obstacles more than rivals, and passing them on straight sections is safer than squeezing by in a bend.

Final verdict

Bright, fast and easy to grasp, it delivers quick, silly races with almost no learning curve. There is little depth, and wins can feel more like luck than skill at first. A good pick for casual players who want short bursts of motion.
