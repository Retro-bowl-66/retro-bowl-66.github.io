Inside Avalanche: Dodge what falls, for as long as you can

Some Flash games needed only one idea, and this 2007 release from The Game Homepage is a clean example. Debris rains down. You stand beneath it. Everything else follows, except that the sky grows steadily more crowded and your sidestepping must sharpen to keep pace.

Gameplay overview

Your little runner shuffles left and right along the ground while objects tumble from above. Taking a hit hurts, so survival is the whole objective, and the score rises with every second you stay upright. Early on the debris is sparse and easy to read. Later, the gaps between falling pieces become the only safe ground.

Beginner's checklist

  • Hover around the middle so either side offers an escape.
  • Keep your eyes on the upper half of the play area, not on your feet.
  • Use short taps of movement rather than long sprints.
  • Spot openings forming early and drift toward them before they close.

Strategy notes

Reading ahead is everything. Once something is near the ground it is too late to react, so plan two or three moves in advance based on what has just appeared. Corners are traps, offering only one exit. As the pace climbs, small early adjustments beat dramatic last-second lunges.

Final verdict

Simple to learn and instant to restart, it does its one job well. Presentation is basic and variety is close to nil, so it rarely holds attention for long. A decent few-minute reflex test and little more.
