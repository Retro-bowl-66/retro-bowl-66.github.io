Inside Backrooms: Lost in the internet's most famous empty office

The legend says that if you slip out of reality in the wrong spot, you land somewhere that looks like an abandoned office designed by nobody: musty carpet, sickly wallpaper and lights that never stop humming. Fancy's 2022 horror game drops you into exactly that place and lets the atmosphere do most of the frightening.

Gameplay overview

You wander a maze of near-identical rooms and corridors, searching for a way out while the sense that you are not alone keeps growing. There is very little guidance, which is the point. Progress comes from noticing small differences in the surroundings, keeping your nerve when a sound comes from nowhere and moving on before you get cornered.

Beginner's checklist

  • Play with sound on, since audio cues often warn you before anything appears.
  • Pick a wall-following rule and stick to it so you do not loop endlessly.
  • Look for anything out of place, because differences in the rooms usually mean progress.
  • Keep moving rather than hiding in corners, where escape routes are limited.

Strategy notes

The maze is designed to disorient, so impose your own structure. Mental landmarks, such as a flickering light or an odd doorway, help you recognize where you have been. When you hear something nearby, move away calmly instead of sprinting blindly into unknown corridors. Panic is the main thing the game is trying to provoke.

Final verdict

The atmosphere is genuinely unsettling and captures the legend's eerie mood. Long stretches of aimless wandering will bore some players, and the scares rely on tension more than events. Recommended for horror fans who prefer dread to jump scares.
