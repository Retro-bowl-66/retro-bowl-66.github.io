Bacon May Die Online

Inside Bacon May Die: A strip of bacon with surprising fighting technique

The title is a pun, the hero is a slice of bacon, and the jokes do not get more sophisticated from there. That would be a problem if the fighting were sloppy, but SnoutUp Games put real care into it. Released in April 2021, this action game hides a combat system that rewards timing beneath its silliness.

Gameplay overview

Stages fill with enemies, and you work through them using regular attacks alongside special moves that hit harder or cover more ground. Waves get busier as you progress, mixing different foes so you cannot rely on a single trick. The goal is to survive each stage and move on, with specials doing the heavy lifting when the screen gets crowded.

Beginner's checklist

  • Learn the basic attack rhythm before leaning on special moves.
  • Save specials for moments when you are surrounded rather than using them immediately.
  • Keep enemies on one side of you so you are never hit from behind.
  • Watch enemy wind-ups, since landing your hit just before theirs avoids damage.

Strategy notes

Crowd positioning wins fights. Move so enemies line up on one side, then attack into the group. Special moves are most valuable as escape tools or crowd clearers, so treat them as a reserve rather than an opener. If a stage keeps beating you, slow down: patient spacing usually beats frantic button pressing.

Final verdict

Underneath the bacon jokes is a combat system with genuine bite, which is a pleasant surprise. The humor is broad and will not suit everyone, and stages can feel samey in longer sessions. Good for players who like quick, crunchy fights with a silly coat of paint.
