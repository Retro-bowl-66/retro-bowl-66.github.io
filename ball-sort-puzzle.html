Inside Ball Sort Puzzle: The tube-sorting puzzle in its plainest form

This is the version the reskins are built on: colored balls, glass tubes and no distractions. Ball sorting became a staple of quick, low-stakes puzzling because it takes seconds to understand and still manages to tie your brain in knots by the later levels. Here it is without seasonal costumes or sports themes.

Gameplay overview

Each level starts with tubes holding a mix of colors. You click a tube's top ball, then click a destination, and the move happens if there is room and the colors agree, or if the target is empty. Finishing means every tube holds one color. More colors and fewer spare tubes push the difficulty up.

Beginner's checklist

  • Start by finding the color that is closest to complete.
  • Use the empty tube as a holding area rather than filling it permanently.
  • Avoid moves that bury a nearly sorted color beneath other balls.
  • When a level looks impossible, count how many moves each color needs.

Control scheme

  • Click – sort

Strategy notes

The trick is sequencing. Before moving a ball, ask what it uncovers and whether that uncovered ball has a place to go. Chains of useful moves beat isolated ones. Late levels often need you to temporarily mess up one tube to unlock another, so accept short-term disorder when it leads somewhere.

Final verdict

Clean, calm and well suited to a few unhurried minutes. It has no story and few surprises, which is both the appeal and the limitation. A solid pick for anyone who likes quiet logic puzzles.
