Basket and Ball Online

Inside Basket and Ball: You are the basketball, and the hoop is far away

Sun Temple's 2015 game turns the usual setup inside out. You are not a player chasing the ball; you are the ball. Each level is an obstacle course you bounce through on your own, scooping up stars before finishing at the net. It borrows from platformers and physics puzzles in roughly equal measure.

Gameplay overview

Rolling is handled by the arrow keys and hopping by the space bar, which is your main tool for clearing gaps and climbing ledges. Levels mix platforms, hazards and awkward angles. Certain stages switch to a shootout where you drag and release to throw instead. Sinking the ball completes the level, and stars reward thoroughness.

Beginner's checklist

  • Get a feel for bounce height on flat ground before tackling gaps.
  • Collect stars on the way rather than going back for them.
  • Slow down before ledges so you do not overshoot.
  • In shootout challenges, adjust aim gradually after each miss.

Control scheme

  • Left / Right Arrow Keys – move
  • Space – bounce
  • Drag – aim
  • Release – shoot
  • Note – only drag and shoot when at Shootout Challenges

Strategy notes

Momentum control is the skill to master. A bounce at speed carries you far, which helps over gaps but sends you off edges. Approach tricky sections slowly and add speed only when you need distance. In shootouts, judge the arc rather than power alone, since a higher, softer throw is often more forgiving than a flat, hard one.

Final verdict

The ball's-eye premise is fun and the mix of genres keeps levels varied. Controls can feel slippery on tight platforms, and the shootouts are less interesting than the courses. A cheerful pick for players who enjoy light platforming.

Details
