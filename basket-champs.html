Inside Basket Champs: A shooting tournament with a restless hoop

RavalMatic's 2016 basketball game skips dribbling and defense and focuses entirely on the shot. You pick a team, enter a tournament and try to outscore each opponent in turn. The catch, which arrives soon enough, is that the basket does not always stay where you left it.

Gameplay overview

Moving the mouse adjusts your shot's angle and arc, and a click sends the ball on its way. Each match is a shooting contest: make more baskets than the other side and you advance through the bracket. At times the hoop moves, which turns a simple aiming game into a timing exercise as well.

Beginner's checklist

  • Practice a consistent arc on the stationary hoop first.
  • When the basket moves, time your release rather than chasing it.
  • Watch your opponent's score so you know how many baskets you need.
  • Prefer a high arc, which forgives small aiming errors.

Control scheme

  • Mouse – aim
  • Click – shoot

Strategy notes

Moving hoops reward rhythm. Watch the basket complete a full cycle, then pick a point on its path to aim at and release as it approaches. Do not adjust aim and timing at once; fix your arc and vary only when you shoot. Consistency over a whole match matters more than spectacular single shots.

Final verdict

Simple controls and short matches make it easy to pick up. Depth is limited to aiming and timing, and the tournament structure gets repetitive. A pleasant few minutes for anyone who enjoys shooting challenges.
