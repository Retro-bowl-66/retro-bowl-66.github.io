Basket Random Online

Inside Basket Random: Floppy basketball where one button does everything

RHM Interactive's 2020 game takes basketball and removes nearly all control from it, which turns out to be the joke. Players flop around like wobbly toys, the ball bounces with a mind of its own, and the court changes from one round to the next. It is built for two people laughing at the same screen.

Gameplay overview

Each player gets just one key, which makes their character jump and swing its arms. Timing that press is everything: hit it at the right moment and you might grab the ball or launch a shot, hit it badly and you tumble over. Points come from getting the ball through the hoop, and the random variations keep any single tactic from dominating.

Beginner's checklist

  • Tap your key in short bursts rather than holding or spamming it.
  • Watch how your player swings before trying to time a shot.
  • Stay near the ball, because rebounds are where most chances come from.
  • Adapt quickly when the court or players change between rounds.

Strategy notes

Chaos is the rule, but timing gives you an edge. Watch the rhythm of your player's wobble and press as their arms swing toward the hoop. Defense is mostly about getting in the way, so position near your basket when the opponent has the ball. Do not fight the random changes; whoever adjusts fastest usually wins.

Final verdict

Brilliant with a friend sitting next to you, and the chaos produces genuinely funny moments. Solo against the computer, the charm wears thin quickly, and skill only goes so far. Best enjoyed as a two-player party game.
