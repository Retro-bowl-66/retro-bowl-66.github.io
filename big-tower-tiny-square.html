Inside Big Tower Tiny Square: A very tall climb for a very small square

Evil Objective's 2018 platformer has a title that doubles as its plot summary. You are a tiny square. The tower is enormous. Something at the top is worth reaching, and between you and it sit sharp spikes, sliding hazards and leaps that leave almost no margin. Failure is constant, but each one costs only seconds.

Gameplay overview

The climb is divided into compact rooms, each built around a pattern you can learn. Clinging to walls and kicking off them matters as much as ordinary jumping, and hazards move on fixed rhythms. Checkpoints stop failure from erasing too much progress, so the ascent becomes a chain of bite-sized tests of execution.

Beginner's checklist

  • Watch each room's hazards for a full cycle before committing.
  • Get comfortable kicking off walls early, since the climb depends on it.
  • Split tricky rooms into sections and master them one piece at a time.
  • Step away briefly after a long streak of deaths to reset your focus.

Strategy notes

Precision platformers reward rhythm. Most rooms hide a timing pattern, and once you see it, the solution is executing that pattern cleanly. Many deaths come from moving a fraction too early, so resist rushing. Against walls, short controlled taps usually beat holding a direction, which tends to fling you past the ledge you wanted.

Final verdict

Tight controls and well-designed rooms make it one of the better precision platformers in a browser. The difficulty will put off casual players, and repeated deaths can grate. Ideal for players who enjoy mastering tough, short challenges.
