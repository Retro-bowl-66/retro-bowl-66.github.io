Bitcoin Clicker Online

Inside Bitcoin Clicker: Mining make-believe coins one click at a time

Julian Yamen's 2018 clicker borrows the vocabulary of cryptocurrency and uses it for an old idea: click to earn, spend to earn faster. It is worth stating plainly that nothing here is real. The coins are virtual, no money is involved, and the only thing you can lose is time.

Gameplay overview

You start by clicking to generate coins, then spend them on upgrades that produce coins automatically. Each purchase raises your income, letting you afford bigger upgrades, and the numbers climb steadily. The goal is open-ended: grow your virtual fortune as large as you can and see how far the upgrade ladder goes.

Beginner's checklist

  • Click actively at the start, since early income comes mostly from you.
  • Buy the first automatic upgrade as soon as you can afford it.
  • Compare upgrades by how much income each adds per coin spent.

Strategy notes

Idle games reward efficiency. Instead of buying the most expensive item available, compare what each upgrade adds relative to its cost and buy the best value. Saving for a big upgrade sometimes pays more than several small ones. Once automatic income outpaces clicking, your attention is better spent planning purchases than tapping.

Final verdict

Simple and steady, it scratches the clicker itch without asking much. The theme is thin and progression gets repetitive, which is true of most games in the genre. Best for idle game fans who like watching numbers grow.
