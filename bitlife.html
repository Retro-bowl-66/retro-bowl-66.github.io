BitLife Online

Inside BitLife: A whole life, one year per click

Candywriter's 2018 life simulator lets you live an entire fictional life in text, from birth to the final line of the obituary. You age a year at a time, and each year brings events, choices and consequences. The results swing between absurd, touching and darkly funny, often within the same lifetime.

Gameplay overview

A big green button moves you forward one year. In between, you decide how to spend your time: school, relationships, jobs, hobbies and decisions that can go very well or very badly. Random events keep things unpredictable. There is no single goal, so the aim is whatever you make it, a long contented life or a gloriously chaotic one.

Beginner's checklist

  • Play your first life without a plan to see what the game throws at you.
  • Keep an eye on stats such as health and happiness as you age.
  • Experiment with different choices in later lives to see new outcomes.
  • Read event text carefully, because small decisions can have long consequences.

Control scheme

  • Click – action

Strategy notes

The interest comes from setting yourself challenges. Try to reach a certain career, live as long as possible or build a particular kind of family. Your stats shape what is available, so investing in education or health early can open doors later. Risky choices create the funniest stories, but they also tend to end lives early.

Final verdict

Clever, funny and highly replayable, it turns small decisions into surprisingly memorable stories. It is all text and very little actual gameplay, which will bore some players. Great for anyone who likes narrative experiments and dark humor.
