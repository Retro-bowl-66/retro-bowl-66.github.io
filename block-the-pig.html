Inside Block the Pig: Fence in a pig that badly wants out

Sun Temple's 2014 puzzle has the kind of premise that sounds too thin to work. A pig sits on a board of tiles. You want it to stay. It wants to leave. You lay one obstacle, it trots one space, and the exchange repeats until the animal is fenced in or has wandered off the side.

Gameplay overview

Every turn you click a tile to drop a barrier, and the pig answers by heading toward whatever exit looks closest. It is not stupid, so a wall in front of its nose only sends it sideways. You need to anticipate its route and seal escape paths before it reaches them. A complete enclosure wins.

Beginner's checklist

  • Start your barrier a few tiles out rather than hugging the animal.
  • After each placement, note which way the pig turns to read its intent.
  • Seal the side nearest an exit before it becomes urgent.
  • Resist dropping tiles right beside the pig in the opening turns.

Control scheme

  • Click – place Block

Strategy notes

Think in terms of a fence rather than a wall. Blocks right in front of the pig just redirect it. A ring some distance away gives you time to close gaps as it wanders. When the pig heads for an opening, block a step ahead of it rather than right next to it, so it has to turn back.

Final verdict

A clever little puzzle with a premise you understand instantly. Some levels feel decided by the opening layout, which can make losses frustrating. Ideal for players who enjoy short, sharp logic challenges.
