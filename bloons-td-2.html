Bloons TD 2 Online

Inside Bloons TD 2: More towers, tougher balloons, same cheerful mayhem

Ninja Kiwi's 2008 sequel did what good sequels do: kept the joke, expanded the toolkit and raised the difficulty. Balloons still follow a track, monkeys and their allies still try to pop them, and every balloon that escapes still hurts. The difference is a wider choice of defenses and balloons that do not go down so easily.

Gameplay overview

Rounds play out the familiar way. You buy towers, place them along the path and let them work, earning money from every pop to fund more defenses. The tougher balloon types demand more thought about which towers you use and where. Clear every round without running out of lives to complete a track.

Beginner's checklist

  • Find the spots where the track overlaps itself and build there first.
  • Mix tower types instead of relying on a single favorite.
  • Expect harder balloons later and leave money for answers.
  • Restart a track with a new layout if the first one collapses.

Strategy notes

With more tools, the challenge shifts from placement to composition. Think about what each tower does well and cover its weaknesses with others. Put area-damage towers where balloons bunch up and long-range ones where they can see a lot of track. Keep a small cash buffer before a tough round so you can respond to leaks.

Final verdict

A clear step up from the original, with more choices and a sharper difficulty curve. It still looks dated beside later entries and lacks their depth. A good pick for players who want classic Bloons without too much complexity.
