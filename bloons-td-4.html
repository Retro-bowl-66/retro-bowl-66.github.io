Bloons TD 4 Online

Inside Bloons TD 4: The entry where Bloons strategy really opened up

Plenty of fans point to the 2009 fourth entry as the moment Ninja Kiwi's series found its stride. The towers multiplied, upgrades branched in more interesting directions and the balloons became varied enough to demand real planning. It keeps the bright, silly charm of its predecessors, with more thinking underneath.

Gameplay overview

You set up monkey towers along the path before each wave and earn money for every balloon popped. That money funds new towers and upgrades, chosen from separate paths for each tower. Balloon types require specific answers, so a balanced defense matters. Rounds escalate steadily, and late waves test whether your setup can handle everything at once.

Beginner's checklist

  • Start with a few cheap towers at key bends, then upgrade them.
  • Read upgrade descriptions to see what each path adds.
  • Prepare for balloons that ignore certain towers before they arrive.
  • Leave space near the path for powerful towers later.

Strategy notes

Upgrades are where this entry rewards planning. A fully upgraded tower in the right spot can outperform several basic ones, but only if it covers a useful stretch of track. Watch for balloon types that slip past your main defenses and add specialist towers ahead of time. Spending wisely between rounds matters more than reacting during them.

Final verdict

Deep, polished for its era and still very playable, it deserves its reputation. The visuals have aged, and newer entries have added more options. A strong choice for anyone who wants tower defense with real decisions.
