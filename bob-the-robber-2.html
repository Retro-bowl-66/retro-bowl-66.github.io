Bob the Robber 2 Online

Inside Bob the Robber 2: Sneaking past guards with a likeable burglar

Meow Beast's 2012 stealth game casts you as Bob, a cheerful thief whose career involves a lot of crouching behind furniture. Each level is a building wired with guards, cameras and alarms, and the prize is somewhere inside. Rushing gets you caught. Watching, waiting and then moving at exactly the right moment gets you paid.

Gameplay overview

You guide Bob through rooms toward his goal while staying out of sight. Guards walk set routes, cameras sweep their arcs and alarms wait to be tripped. Hiding spots let you wait out a patrol, and switches and doors open new paths. A level is done when Bob reaches the target and gets away clean.

Beginner's checklist

  • Watch a guard's full route before moving into its path.
  • Hide whenever you are unsure rather than guessing.
  • Look for ways to switch off cameras before passing them.
  • Move in short, quick bursts from one safe spot to the next.

Strategy notes

Stealth is about timing windows. Every patrol and camera has a cycle, and the safe moment to move is usually short. Count the beats, then go. Decide where you will hide next before leaving cover, so you are never caught in the open. Disabling a camera often saves more time than dodging it repeatedly.

Final verdict

Charming characters and tidy level design make the stealth approachable rather than stressful. Levels can feel slow while you wait for patrols, and some puzzles slip into trial and error. A good introduction to stealth puzzles.
