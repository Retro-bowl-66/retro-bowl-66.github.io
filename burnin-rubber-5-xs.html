Burnin' Rubber 5 XS Online

Inside Burnin' Rubber 5 XS: Racing where the car in front is also a target

XformGames combines two old pleasures in this 2020 racer: going fast and blowing up the car ahead of you. Built in Unity WebGL, it looks polished for a browser title, with shiny cars, varied tracks and explosions to match. Underneath the weaponry, though, it rewards the same skill as any racer, which is clean driving.

Gameplay overview

The arrow keys drive, while Z and X fire your two weapons. Hitting rivals can slow them down or knock them off line, but a missed shot does nothing for you while a missed corner costs you plenty. Tracks vary in layout and surface, and the best results come from tidy cornering plus well-chosen moments to attack.

Beginner's checklist

  • Focus on driving the racing line before worrying about weapons.
  • Fire at rivals on straights, where aiming is easier.
  • Save one weapon for defending your position near the finish.
  • Learn each track's tight corners on early laps.

Control scheme

  • Arrow Keys – drive
  • Z – weapon 1
  • X – weapon 2

Strategy notes

Weapons are a bonus, not a strategy. A driver who takes corners cleanly will beat one who fires constantly and crashes. Attack when a rival is directly ahead on a straight, or when you need to break up a pack. Near the end of a race, protect your lead instead of chasing more hits.

Final verdict

Slick visuals and punchy combat give it plenty of appeal. The racing itself is fairly standard, and weapon spam from rivals can feel frustrating. A good pick for anyone who wants some action with their laps.
