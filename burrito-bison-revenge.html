Inside Burrito Bison Revenge: The masked wrestler's second round of launching

Juicy Beast's 2012 follow-up sends its masked wrestler back into the candy world with a fresh set of upgrades and challenges. The basic idea has not shifted: you are launched forward, and the goal is to travel as far as possible by bouncing off whatever stands in the way. More things to buy make the progression loop richer.

Gameplay overview

Each run starts with a launch, then you keep the bison airborne by smashing into obstacles and enemies along the route. Abilities give extra bursts of speed or bounce when you need them. Coins collected during the run fund upgrades between attempts, and new challenges reward particular feats. Each launch carries him a little further as the upgrades stack up.

Beginner's checklist

  • Upgrade the launch first so every run starts strongly.
  • Time your actions to hit enemies and obstacles that give big bounces.
  • Use abilities when your speed starts to fade, not right away.
  • Accept that early runs will be short while upgrades accumulate.

Strategy notes

Think of each run as an investment. Spend early coins on upgrades that affect every run, such as launch strength, before buying situational extras. During the run, aim your timing at bouncy targets and avoid flat stretches where you lose speed. Abilities are rescues, so keep one in reserve for when momentum is about to die.

Final verdict

Satisfying and easy to understand, it refines its predecessor's loop without changing much. Grinding for upgrades can drag in the middle. A good choice for fans of the series and of launcher games in general.
