Inside Burrito Bison: A luchador, a launch and a land of candy

Juicy Beast's launcher stars a masked wrestler with a grudge and a world full of candy-coated creatures who have no idea what is about to hit them. The bison is flung forward at the start of each run and bounces off whatever he lands on. Distance is the score, and every run feeds the next with cash to spend.

Gameplay overview

After the launch, you keep the bison moving by timing actions as he lands on candy creatures, each bounce adding momentum. Special abilities give extra boosts when the run starts to sag. When he finally slides to a stop, the money collected goes into upgrades that make the next launch longer. Progress builds steadily over many short runs.

Beginner's checklist

  • Time your actions to land on creatures rather than the ground.
  • Spend early money on upgrades that improve launch distance.
  • Hold special abilities for moments when speed drops.
  • Expect early runs to be short, since progress comes from repetition.

Strategy notes

Momentum is everything. A run lives or dies on how often you land on something bouncy instead of skidding along the floor, so keep an eye on what is ahead. Upgrades that extend your initial launch or improve bounce strength usually pay for themselves fastest. Abilities are best used to rescue a sagging run, not at full speed.

Final verdict

Silly, colorful and steadily rewarding, it makes good use of the upgrade loop. Runs become repetitive once the novelty fades, and progress can feel grindy midway. A fun pick for players who enjoy launcher games.
