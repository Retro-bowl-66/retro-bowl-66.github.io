Inside Cannon Basketball 4: Basketball by cannon, with physics as the referee

Oleg Kuzyk's 2018 puzzle replaces the jump shot with artillery. The ball sits in a cannon, the net hangs somewhere inconvenient, and the space between is cluttered with ramps, blockers and parts that swing or slide. Each stage is a small physics riddle whose answer is one well-chosen trajectory.

Gameplay overview

You tilt the barrel and set the strength, then fire and watch. The ball arcs, ricochets and reacts to the contraptions around it in a believable way. Straight-line solutions are rare, so expect to use a wall, a slope or a moving piece on most stages. Judging success is easy: the ball either drops through or it does not.

Beginner's checklist

  • Take one exploratory shot to see how the arc behaves.
  • Scan for walls and slopes that could steer the ball toward the net.
  • Change the angle or the strength, never both at once.
  • Fire in step with moving pieces rather than against them.

Strategy notes

Reverse-engineer each stage. Start at the net and ask what surface could feed the ball into it, then find an arc that reaches that surface. Swinging and sliding parts follow cycles, so fire in rhythm with them rather than trying to blast through. When an attempt nearly works, nudge one setting slightly instead of starting from scratch.

Final verdict

Clever level design and clear physics make it a satisfying puzzle. Some levels rely on precise timing that feels more like luck, and the presentation is plain. A good fit for players who enjoy trajectory puzzles.
