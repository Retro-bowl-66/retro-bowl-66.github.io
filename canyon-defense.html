Canyon Defense Online

Inside Canyon Defense: Defending a gorge where the cliffs choose your spots

Miniclip's 2008 defense game takes place among towering cliffs, and the geography matters more than usual. Attackers squeeze through slots between the rock walls, and the land itself limits where you can build. That makes every placement feel weighty, because the good spots are few and the bad ones are expensive mistakes.

Gameplay overview

Towers go down beside the route, and the cash from each wave buys upgrades or new ones. Attackers differ in pace and toughness, and the later groups come in larger numbers. Since the cliffs allow only a handful of strong sites, securing them early and investing heavily there is the heart of the game.

Beginner's checklist

  • Scout for building spots that overlook more than one stretch of the route.
  • Grab the strongest sites early, before the pressure arrives.
  • Invest in towers on good ground rather than scattering weak ones.
  • Combine tower types so both quick and sturdy attackers are covered.

Strategy notes

Coverage is where value comes from. One tower that sees two sections of the route beats two towers that each see one. Put your heaviest firepower at the bends and keep improving it. A small cash reserve protects you when a sudden rush of fast attackers slips past a setup built for slow ones.

Final verdict

The terrain gives the strategy a distinctive shape, and the challenge ramps up fairly. Visuals have aged, and once you know the best sites, replays turn predictable. A solid pick for fans of classic tower defense.
