Inside Cartoon Mini Racing: Chunky toy cars on twisty little circuits

Brain Software's 2018 racer looks like a toy box tipped onto a race circuit. The cars are chunky and bright, the courses twist back on themselves, and a race is over before you have time to get bored. Behind the cartoon style sits a reasonably sharp little racer that punishes lazy braking and rewards curiosity.

Gameplay overview

You steer with the arrow keys against rival cars around compact circuits. Bends arrive in quick succession, and taking them cleanly matters more than raw speed. Some circuits conceal alternate routes that save precious seconds once you know where they are. Flip over or wedge yourself against a wall and the H key drops you back on the road.

Beginner's checklist

  • Run a lap or two purely to memorize where each bend sits.
  • Lift off the throttle ahead of sharp turns rather than mid-corner.
  • Keep an eye out for openings at the track edge that could save time.
  • Reset with H the moment you are wedged, since reversing wastes more time.

Control scheme

  • Arrow Keys – drive
  • H – reset Car

Strategy notes

With races this brief, a single crash is hard to recover from, so tidy driving wins. A clean lap without resets usually beats a reckless one. Once a circuit is familiar, try a suspected alternate route in a race that is already lost, so a failed experiment costs nothing. Watching where rivals turn in can also hint at the quickest line.

Final verdict

Cheerful, brisk and simple to grasp, with a little extra depth hidden in the alternate routes. The selection of circuits is small, and the computer drivers are not very cunning. A pleasant option for light, low-stakes racing.
