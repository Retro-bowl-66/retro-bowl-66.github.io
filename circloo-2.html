Inside circloO 2: Reach a circle and the world grows larger

The 2018 sequel to Florian van Strien's physics puzzle keeps the geometric look and adds a structure that keeps unfolding. You steer a ball toward rings placed around a level, and touching one makes the playfield grow, exposing a new stretch of hazards. What begins as a cramped space gradually becomes a sprawling puzzle.

Gameplay overview

Movement uses the arrow keys or WASD, and Esc pauses. Every ring in a level must be touched, with each one unlocking another section. Obstacles involve physics, timing and sometimes moving pieces that respond to your ball. Touch the final ring and the stage is cleared, ready for the next one.

Beginner's checklist

  • Explore each newly revealed area before rushing to the next circle.
  • Use slopes and objects to build speed for difficult sections.
  • Pause and plan when a new area looks complicated.

Control scheme

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move
  • Esc / Back – pause

Strategy notes

The expanding structure means early decisions can affect later sections. Look at how new areas connect to old ones, since a route may run back through ground you have already covered. Momentum matters as much as in the first game, so think about where to build speed. Patience pays off on the larger levels.

Final verdict

A smart evolution of the original, with levels that feel bigger and more surprising. Some sections demand precise control, and the minimal look will not win everyone over. Ideal for fans of the first game and of physics puzzles in general.

Details

  • Studio: Florian van Strien
  • Category: Puzzle Games
  • Launched: May 2018
  • Price: Free browser version
