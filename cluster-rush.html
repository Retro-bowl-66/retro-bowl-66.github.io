Inside Cluster Rush: Running across a convoy at highway speed

SlashRoll's 2015 game turns a motorway convoy into an obstacle course. Your feet are on the roof of a moving lorry, the next lorry is a leap away, and the one you want is all the way at the front. Missing a jump drops you onto the tarmac and resets the attempt. The rush of motion is half the reason to play.

Gameplay overview

Played in first person, each level is a column of trucks moving at speed, and you run and jump from roof to roof. Trucks shift position and do not all behave alike, so a clean route requires reading the convoy as well as timing your leaps. Reaching the front truck finishes the stage.

Beginner's checklist

  • Watch how each truck moves before committing to a jump.
  • Take off from the very edge of a roof for maximum distance.
  • Avoid jumping toward trucks that are drifting away from you.
  • Look several trucks ahead so the next gap never surprises you.

Strategy notes

Momentum is your friend until it is not. A running jump covers far more ground than a standing one, but it is harder to control on landing. Aim for the middle of the next roof rather than its edge. When trucks are shifting lanes, wait a moment for a gap to close instead of attempting a long diagonal leap.

Final verdict

The speed and first person view make every level feel lively, and the core idea is great fun. Controls can feel slippery, and some falls seem unfair. A strong pick for anyone who enjoys parkour and quick, repeatable challenges.
