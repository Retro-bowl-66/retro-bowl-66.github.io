Contact

The way to reach Retro-Bowl-66 is by email at contact@retro-bowl-66.github.io. There is no contact form, account system or comment section, so email is the one channel we use.

What to include

  • The address of the page you are writing about, or at least the game's name.
  • What happened, in a sentence or two. For a broken game, describe what you saw: a blank frame, an error message, frozen controls.
  • Your browser and device, such as Chrome on a Windows laptop or Safari on an iPhone.
  • For rights issues, details showing that you own or represent the work.

What you can write to us about

  • Games that do not load, run badly or have stopped working.
  • Mistakes in a guide, such as wrong controls or an outdated description.
  • Accessibility problems anywhere on the site.
  • Requests from developers to remove, update or credit a game.
  • Copyright notices; our DMCA page lists what a formal notice needs.
  • Privacy questions or requests about your personal data.
  • Questions about the ads shown on the site.

A short, specific message is easier to act on than a long one. If you are reporting several broken games, a simple list of page addresses works well. Messages are handled in the order they arrive. We cannot promise a personal reply to every one, but reports about broken games and errors are always looked at. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information by email.
