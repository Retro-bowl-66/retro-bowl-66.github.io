Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Related technologies, such as local storage, do a similar job in a different way. This page explains which of them Retro-Bowl-66 uses and how you can control them.

Analytics cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies that help us count visits, see which pages people open and learn which devices and browsers they use. The results reach us as aggregate reports, not as a profile of any one visitor. You can block Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising cookies

Google AdSense and its partners use cookies to show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure how ads perform and, where you allow it, personalize ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com or www.aboutads.info/choices. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before these cookies are used for personalized ads.

Storage set by games

The games come from third-party developers and run inside an embedded frame. Many of them use cookies, local storage or similar browser storage to keep your progress, high scores and settings. That storage belongs to the game's own domain, and its developer decides what goes in it. Clearing it will usually reset your saves, so keep that in mind before wiping site data.

Managing cookies

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies, usually under privacy or site data in its settings. You can choose to:

  • block third-party cookies while keeping first-party ones;
  • delete cookies automatically when you close the browser;
  • clear stored data for a single site without touching the rest.

Blocking all cookies and storage may stop some games from saving progress, and a few may not load at all.
