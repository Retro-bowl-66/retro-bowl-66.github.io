Crazy Cars Online

Inside Crazy Cars: Pick a car, find a ramp, ignore the rules

No Pressure Studios clearly took its own name to heart. This Unity WebGL driving game has no strict objectives, no timer breathing down your neck and no lecture about racing lines. You choose a car, roll into an open environment and spend your time racing around, testing jumps or seeing how badly a drift can go.

Gameplay overview

After selecting a vehicle, you accelerate and steer around the map, which is scattered with ramps and wide spaces made for stunts. Different cars handle differently, so swapping between them changes the feel of the same terrain. With no fixed goals, structure comes from your own experiments.

Beginner's checklist

  • Try a few different cars to find one whose handling you like.
  • Use open areas to practice drifts before attempting them near obstacles.
  • Hit ramps straight on for cleaner, longer jumps.

Strategy notes

Make your own challenges. Time a lap around a landmark, try to land a jump without rolling or chain a drift through several turns. Heavier-feeling cars tend to be steadier on landings, while lighter ones are easier to throw around corners. Learning these differences gives the free roaming a sense of progress.

Final verdict

Easygoing and decent-looking for a browser title, it rewards anyone who likes to tinker with handling. Players who need objectives will run out of reasons to stay fairly quickly. Think of it as a toy rather than a game.
