Crazy Tunnel 3D Online

Inside Crazy Tunnel 3D: A bright tube, high speed and things in the way

Released in May 2022 and built in Unity WebGL, this reaction game shoves you down a bright, twisting tunnel at a speed that discourages careful thought. Obstacles appear in your path, and your only options are to dash sideways or leap over them. It is short, sharp and designed to be retried immediately.

Gameplay overview

The left and right keys throw you sideways across the tube, and up or W hops you over trouble. Hazards arrive in clusters, and bends in the tunnel can conceal the next set until the last moment. Staying alive is the whole objective, with distance as the yardstick. There is barely time to plan, so fast reading and tidy inputs carry you furthest.

Beginner's checklist

  • Keep to the middle of the tunnel so you can dash either way.
  • Jump only when a dash will not work, since landings take time.
  • Watch further ahead on curves where obstacles are hidden.

Control scheme

  • Left / Right Arrows – dash Left / Right
  • Up Arrow / W Key – jump

Strategy notes

Obstacle types are few enough to learn. Pay attention to which ones demand a jump and which a dash, and respond with the same input every time. Avoid panicking into double moves, which often put you into a second obstacle. Calm, single responses survive longer than frantic ones.

Final verdict

Fast, bright and easy to pick up, it offers a quick shot of reflex play. It lacks variety and depth, so it tends not to hold attention for long. Fine for a few runs at a time.
