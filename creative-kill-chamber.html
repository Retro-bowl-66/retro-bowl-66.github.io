Inside Creative Kill Chamber: Stick figure puzzles that reward odd ideas

Stick Page's 2010 Flash game sets up a series of rooms and asks you to be inventive with whatever each one contains. There is rarely one obvious answer. The game rewards poking at objects, combining them and seeing what happens, and its dark stick-figure humor is part of the package.

Gameplay overview

Each chamber is a self-contained puzzle with a handful of items and features. You interact with them, test ideas and watch the results unfold. Some approaches work, many do not, and the fun lies partly in the failures. Solving a chamber moves you on to the next, with fresh tools and new problems.

Beginner's checklist

  • Click on every object in a chamber to see what it does.
  • Try combining items in ways that seem unlikely.
  • Watch each result fully, since outcomes can reveal clues.

Strategy notes

Experimentation is the whole strategy. Make a quick mental list of what each chamber contains, then try pairings systematically rather than at random. If an idea almost works, look for a small change in timing or order. The game is designed to reward curiosity, so an unexpected idea is often the right one.

Final verdict

Inventive and darkly funny, it makes good use of a simple premise. Some solutions feel arbitrary, and the presentation is basic Flash-era fare. A good fit for players who like quirky puzzles with grim humor.
