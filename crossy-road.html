Inside Crossy Road: The chicken joke, turned into a dare

Hipster Whale's 2014 hit is built on the oldest joke in the book, and it has the timing to justify it. Your chunky little chicken faces an infinite stack of roads, rivers and railway lines, and the only direction worth going is forward. One hop at a time, you push your luck until a car, a log or a train finally calls time.

Gameplay overview

Every input moves you a single space, forward, back or sideways. Roads bring traffic at different speeds, rivers require you to hop across floating logs and railway tracks give a warning before something very fast arrives. Distance is your score. Dawdle too long and an eagle swoops in, so there is no camping in safety.

Beginner's checklist

  • Pause briefly on safe grass to read the next lane.
  • Look for the train warning before stepping onto tracks.
  • Hop onto logs in the river rather than waiting for a perfect line.
  • Keep moving forward steadily to avoid the eagle.

Strategy notes

Rhythm beats speed. Rushing forward invites collisions, but waiting too long brings the eagle, so find a steady pace that lets you read each lane. On roads, cross behind cars rather than in front of them. In rivers, logs drift sideways, so plan where you will hop off before you hop on.

Final verdict

Charming, simple and very well designed, it earned its enormous popularity. Runs are short and the challenge is mostly reflex, so long sessions can feel repetitive. Excellent for quick bursts of play.
