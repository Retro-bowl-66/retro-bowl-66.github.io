Cubefield Online

Inside Cubefield: A tiny ship and a field of unforgiving cubes

Nils Asejevs put this stark little reflex test online in 2006, and it went on to become one of the early web's best-known time sinks. A tiny triangle skims across a flat plain studded with blocks. Touch none of them. That is the entire brief, and the bare visuals only sharpen your focus.

Gameplay overview

Your only inputs are left and right, and the craft keeps gaining speed. Blocks start as loose scatters, then tighten into clusters and walls with slim openings. Survive longer and the pace climbs, while the color scheme shifts in ways that make the field trickier to read. Brushing one block finishes the attempt.

Beginner's checklist

  • Hold a central line so either side remains an option.
  • Scan deep into the field for openings instead of the closest block.
  • Tap the keys briefly rather than holding them for long sweeps.

Strategy notes

Speed makes everything harder to read, so focus your eyes on the distance and steer toward openings early. Small adjustments are safer than big swerves, which can drift you into a cube you did not see. When the formations become walls, commit to a gap rather than hesitating between two.

Final verdict

A classic for good reason, with a clean concept and steadily rising tension. The minimalism means little variety, and runs can end abruptly. Still a sharp test of reflexes.

Details
