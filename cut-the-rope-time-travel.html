Cut the Rope Time Travel Online

Inside Cut the Rope Time Travel: Two Om Noms, several eras and twice the candy

ZeptoLab's 2013 entry gives the series its most playful setup yet. Om Nom travels back through different historical periods, and in each era he meets an ancestor with the same sweet tooth. That premise is more than decoration. Many levels ask you to feed two creatures at once, which changes how you think about every cut.

Gameplay overview

Cutting and clicking work as before, with physics carrying the candy through each level. Each era introduces its own objects and mechanics, adding fresh tools to the familiar set. When two Om Noms are waiting, you must split your attention between two deliveries. Stars still reward precision. The physics behaves exactly as earlier games taught you.

Beginner's checklist

  • Identify which Om Nom is harder to reach and plan around that one.
  • Learn each era's new objects in its early levels.
  • Watch both pieces of candy when two are in play.
  • Chase stars only after you are sure both deliveries work.

Control scheme

  • Drag – cut
  • click – action

Strategy notes

Two targets mean two plans. Decide the order of cuts so that one delivery does not sabotage the other. New era mechanics usually follow a simple rule, so test them in isolation before combining them. When candy must travel far, use the swing and special objects together rather than relying on one.

Final verdict

Inventive and well designed, with the two-target levels adding real variety. Some puzzles demand fiddly timing, and the series formula is familiar by now. One of the stronger entries for players who already enjoy Cut the Rope.
