Inside Dante: Short levels built around careful jumping

Salvatore Previti's 2022 platformer has modest ambitions and a clear focus. Forget sprawling stories or crafting menus. What matters is footwork: sprinting, leaping and touching down exactly on the spot you aimed for. Each stage poses a route-finding question through hazards, and the reward is answering it with a smooth run.

Gameplay overview

You guide your character through levels lined with hazards, picking a path and timing jumps to avoid them. First attempts at a section are usually about learning where the danger lies, and later attempts refine the run. Progress comes from small improvements rather than sudden breakthroughs. Restarts are quick, which keeps the trial and error from dragging.

Beginner's checklist

  • Treat your first try at a section as reconnaissance.
  • Watch for hazards that move or appear on a timer.
  • Aim for clean, deliberate jumps rather than rushed ones.

Strategy notes

Platformers like this reward patience. Pause at safe spots to look ahead before committing to a sequence. Most hazards have a rhythm, and learning it turns a hard section into a routine. If one jump keeps failing, check your takeoff point, since starting a step earlier or later often makes the difference.

Final verdict

Tidy and focused, it delivers solid platforming without fuss. It is also fairly anonymous in style and short on surprises. A reasonable pick for fans of the genre who want something straightforward.
