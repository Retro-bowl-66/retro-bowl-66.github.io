Dead Again Online

Inside Dead Again: Top-down shooting against enemies who keep coming back

The title is the premise. In Lax Viking Games' 2022 action RPG, the enemies you put down have a habit of getting back up, and the challenge is surviving long enough to become strong enough that it no longer matters. It plays as a top-down shooter with a progression system layered on top.

Gameplay overview

Steering is on WASD or the arrows, the mouse handles aim and fire, and R or space refills your magazine. Every kill feeds an experience bar, and each new level hands you an upgrade. Waves thicken as the run goes on, so survival rests on where you stand and on the perks you pick along the way.

Beginner's checklist

  • Keep moving while shooting so enemies cannot surround you.
  • Reload in quiet moments rather than mid-fight.
  • Choose upgrades that strengthen your main weapon early.
  • Watch for enemies getting back up after you defeat them.

Control scheme

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move
  • R / Space – reload
  • Click – shoot

Strategy notes

Treat every fallen enemy as temporary. Keep space between yourself and downed foes, since they may rise again close by. Early upgrades should focus on killing power, which shortens every fight. Later, mobility or survivability helps more as numbers grow. Circling the arena while firing keeps threats in front of you.

Final verdict

Solid shooting and a clear upgrade loop make it easy to get into. Runs can feel similar after a while, and the art is functional rather than memorable. A decent choice for players who enjoy top-down survival shooters.
