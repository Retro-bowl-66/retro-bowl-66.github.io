Dino Bros Online

Inside Dino Bros: Two dinosaurs, a pile of coins and no swimming

Martin Magni's puzzle game gives you two dinosaurs and one shared problem: every coin must be collected and both of them must reach the exit door. The levels are small but cleverly arranged, and the rule that keeps tripping people up is simple. If either dinosaur ends up in the water, the attempt is over.

Gameplay overview

You move with the arrow keys or WASD, guiding the pair through levels full of obstacles and gaps. Coins are scattered in places that often require careful positioning to reach, and the door only counts once all of them are gone. Keeping both dinosaurs safe while chasing coins is where the puzzle lives.

Beginner's checklist

  • Look at where every coin sits before making your first move.
  • Keep track of both dinosaurs, since losing either one ends the attempt.
  • Collect the hardest coin first while the level is still open.

Control scheme

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move

Strategy notes

Treat the two dinosaurs as one puzzle rather than two. A move that helps one may put the other in danger, so check both before acting. Water is the main hazard, so plan routes that avoid edges whenever possible. When a level seems impossible, try a different order of coins.

Final verdict

Charming, compact and smart, it is a good example of a simple idea done well. Some levels feel fiddly, and the visuals are basic. A nice fit for puzzle fans who like short, tricky levels.
