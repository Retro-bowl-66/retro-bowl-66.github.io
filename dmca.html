DMCA Policy

Retro-Bowl-66 respects the rights of creators. If you believe material on retro-bowl-66.github.io infringes your copyright, you can send a notice under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act to contact@retro-bowl-66.github.io.

What a notice must contain

  • Your contact details: full name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The exact URL of each page on retro-bowl-66.github.io where the material appears.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

A notice missing any of these may take longer to handle, since we will need to ask for the missing details. Once we receive a complete notice, we remove or disable access to the material and, where possible, let the responsible party know.

Counter-notices

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same address. It should identify the removed material and where it appeared, include a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed in error, give your name and contact details, state that you consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court, and carry your signature. We may pass the counter-notice to the person who filed the original notice and restore the material if they do not start legal action within the period the law allows.

Simple change requests

Not every concern needs a legal notice. If you made a game listed here and would like it removed, credited differently or updated to a newer version, or you have spotted an error in its guide, just email us with the page address and what you would like changed. We treat these as ordinary requests, with no formal wording required.

Please note that sending a false or misleading notice can carry legal consequences. Consider whether the use might be permitted, for example as fair use, before you file.
