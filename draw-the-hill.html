Inside Draw the Hill: You draw the road, the vehicle does the rest

Stelennnn's 2020 physics toy hands you a pen instead of a steering wheel. A little vehicle trundles forward on its own, and the only way to help it is to sketch the ground beneath its wheels. Hills, bridges and ramps exist only if you draw them, which turns each stage into a quick exercise in amateur civil engineering.

Gameplay overview

Your line becomes solid terrain the moment you draw it, and the vehicle rolls along whatever shape you created. Too steep and it stalls or flips; too flat and it may never clear a gap. Reaching the goal means sketching a route with the right curves and slopes. Mistakes are instant and often funny, so redrawing is part of the rhythm.

Beginner's checklist

  • Draw gentle slopes first and steepen them only when needed.
  • Leave the vehicle a smooth landing after any jump you create.
  • Watch where it hesitates, since that usually marks a bad curve.

Strategy notes

Momentum is the hidden variable. A downhill section drawn before a gap gives the vehicle the speed to cross it, while a sharp bump can launch it off course. Keep transitions smooth, because sudden angle changes are what usually cause flips. When a design fails, adjust one segment rather than erasing everything.

Final verdict

Charming and inventive, it rewards a little creativity with satisfying results. Precision drawing with a mouse can be fiddly, and the challenge stays fairly light. A pleasant pick for relaxed tinkering.
