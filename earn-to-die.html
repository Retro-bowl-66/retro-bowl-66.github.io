Earn to Die Online

Inside Earn to Die: Drive further, earn more, armor up, try again

Toffee Games' 2013 hit runs on a loop that is very hard to put down. The undead have taken over, safety lies somewhere across a ruined desert, and the jalopy you start with is laughably unfit for the trip. Never mind. Every doomed attempt pays out cash, and bit by bit that wreck becomes a plated, rocket-assisted plow.

Gameplay overview

You accelerate, brake and tilt the car to keep it balanced over ramps, wrecks and crowds of the undead. Zombies slow you down, and fuel runs out eventually. When the run ends, you spend your earnings on upgrades such as engines, armor, fuel tanks and mounted weapons. Each improvement pushes the next attempt a little further.

Beginner's checklist

  • Buy fuel upgrades early, since running dry ends most first runs.
  • Tilt to land jumps flat and keep your speed.
  • Avoid big zombie crowds until your car has armor.
  • Save the boost for long, flat stretches of open road.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – forward
  • Down Arrow – backward
  • Left / Right Arrows – tilt
  • Esc – pause
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Upgrade order matters. Fuel capacity and engine power usually give the biggest early gains, while weapons and armor shine once runs get longer. Keep your car level in the air, because nose-first landings bleed speed and damage the vehicle. When progress stalls, consider buying a better car rather than maxing the current one.

Final verdict

Addictive, well paced and very satisfying as your car grows stronger. The structure is repetitive by design, and later stages require some grinding. A great pick for anyone who enjoys upgrade-driven progress.
