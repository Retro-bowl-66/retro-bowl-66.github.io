Edge Let's Surf Online

Inside Edge Let's Surf: The browser's hidden surfing game, out in the open

Microsoft tucked this endless runner inside its Edge browser as a hidden extra, and it turned out to be better than a hidden extra has any right to be. You ride a surfboard down a bright, cartoonish stretch of ocean, steering around obstacles and scooping up items. It is short on fuss and long on charm.

Gameplay overview

You steer left and right to dodge hazards and gather pickups, and the pace quickens over time. Different modes change the objective, from simply going as far as possible to more structured challenges. Collisions slow you down or end the run, so threading a clean line through the obstacles is the core skill.

Beginner's checklist

  • Try each mode once to see which style of run suits you.
  • Keep your board pointed toward open water, not cluttered patches.
  • Grab items when they are on your line rather than swerving for them.

Strategy notes

Smooth lines beat sharp turns. Big swerves cost time and can steer you into a second hazard, so make small, early corrections. Plan two obstacles ahead whenever the screen allows. Items are tempting, but a detour that risks a collision is rarely worth it, especially once the speed has built up.

Final verdict

Bright, responsive and more substantial than its hidden-extra origins suggest. It is still a simple endless runner, and long sessions feel repetitive. A cheerful choice for quick runs.

Details
