Inside Edge Not Found: A puzzle about what happens at the screen's border

Its name plays on an error message you have probably seen too often, and its puzzles play with a part of the screen nobody looks at: the border. Auroriax released it in 2020, and every level treats the limits of the play area as something to experiment with rather than a wall to bump against. Working out each rule is most of the challenge.

Gameplay overview

Each level is a small space where the rules about borders are not what you expect. You explore, test what happens at the edges and use that knowledge to reach the goal. Some solutions may involve leaving one side to arrive somewhere else, while others depend on details you only notice after a few attempts.

Beginner's checklist

  • Walk into every edge of a new level to see how it behaves.
  • Treat the screen border as a tool, not a barrier.
  • Restart and look again when a level seems impossible.

Strategy notes

Assume nothing about boundaries. The key insight in many levels is that an edge may behave differently from what the previous level taught you. Once you understand a level's rule, plan your route around it. When stuck, list the edges you have not tested yet, since one of them is usually the answer.

Final verdict

Clever and conceptual, it rewards players who enjoy bending rules in their head. It is short, and its minimal presentation will not appeal to everyone. A good pick for puzzle fans who like a twist on perception.
