Inside Endless Truck: Three missions, one run, one flip-happy truck

Famobi's 2016 physics driver adds a twist to the familiar bumpy-terrain formula: every level comes with three missions, and all of them must be completed in a single run. That small rule changes everything. You cannot simply survive to the finish; you have to perform specific feats along the way, often while barely keeping the truck upright.

Gameplay overview

The truck rolls forward on its own, and the left and right arrows tilt it while the space bar makes it jump. Tilting in the air lets you pull off flips, which are often part of a mission. Terrain is rough and uneven, so landing well is as important as anything you do in the air.

Beginner's checklist

  • Check the mission list carefully before you set off.
  • Rehearse flips on the gentler early courses first.
  • Decide in advance which stretch of road suits each mission.

Control scheme

  • Left Arrow – tilt Left
  • Right Arrow – tilt Right
  • Space – jump

Strategy notes

Treat each level as a checklist. Decide which mission to tackle first, usually the hardest, so a failure costs less time. Flips need height, so look for big jumps and use them. Landing both wheels together preserves speed and control, which matters when missions require you to keep going after a trick.

Final verdict

The mission structure gives each level purpose and replay value. Physics can feel inconsistent, and failing a mission late in a run is frustrating. A good pick for players who enjoy stunts with specific goals.
