Inside Factory Balls Forever: Decorate a plain ball by doing things in order

The Factory Balls games have always been about sequence, and AI Doge's 2020 entry continues that tradition. A blank ball arrives, a target design is shown, and you have a row of tools to get from one to the other. The trick is that each step can undo or block another, so the order you choose matters as much as the tools themselves.

Gameplay overview

Tools include paint, masks and other gadgets that cover, color or alter parts of the ball. Applying a mask before painting protects one area, while removing it afterward reveals the color underneath. Getting the target pattern means planning a sequence of steps, often thinking backward from the final look. A wrong move can ruin a ball and force a restart.

Beginner's checklist

  • Study the target design before touching any tool.
  • Work out which areas need protecting before you paint.
  • Think backward from the final pattern to find the first step.
  • Restart without hesitation when a step goes wrong.

Strategy notes

Layering is the core concept. Colors applied later sit on top of earlier ones unless something protects them, so paint the areas that need protecting first, then cover them before adding other colors. Break complex designs into regions and handle each in turn. If a solution keeps failing, check whether a mask went on too early or came off too late.

Final verdict

Clever, clean and satisfying, it captures what made the series so enjoyable. Some later puzzles can feel more like trial and error than logic. A great pick for anyone who enjoys sequencing puzzles.
