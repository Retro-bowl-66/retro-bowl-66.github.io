Fruit Ninja Online

Inside Fruit Ninja: Swipe through flying fruit, never the bombs

Halfbrick Studios' 2010 hit turned a swipe of the finger into one of the most recognizable gestures in mobile gaming. Fruit is tossed into the air, and you slash through it before it falls. This browser version reproduces that idea with the mouse, and the Fruit Ninja brand remains with its owner. The satisfying splat is intact.

Gameplay overview

Dragging across the screen draws a blade that slices any fruit it touches. Fruit comes up in waves, sometimes singly and sometimes in clusters, and slicing several in one stroke earns combo points. Bombs appear among the fruit, and hitting one is a disaster. In the classic mode, letting too much fruit fall uncut also ends the run.

Beginner's checklist

  • Use long sweeping strokes that cut through several pieces together.
  • Glance around for any bombs before every single slash.
  • Let fruit rise to the peak of its arc, where it hangs almost still.

Control scheme

  • Drag – slice

Strategy notes

Combos come from patience. Fruit is easiest to group at the top of its flight, so wait a beat and slice several in one motion. Keep swipes away from bombs by curving around them. In busy waves, prioritize fruit that is about to fall off the screen over fruit still rising.

Final verdict

Instantly satisfying and easy to pick up, it captures the original's appeal well. With a mouse it is slightly less tactile than on a touch screen, and sessions are short. A fun choice for quick bursts of slicing.
