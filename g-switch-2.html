G-Switch 2 Online

Inside G-Switch 2: The gravity runner returns with meaner tracks

Serius Games revived its sprinting robot in 2015, half a decade after the first run, and kept the single-key idea while tightening the screws everywhere else. The tracks are fresh and considerably less forgiving. Gravity still flips between floor and ceiling at your command, but the room for error has shrunk and the hazards are more inventive.

Gameplay overview

Any key reverses gravity and Esc pauses. Obstacles now appear on both surfaces in trickier combinations, so landing safely often means flipping at very precise moments. Courses build into longer, more demanding sequences, and success depends on remembering and reading the layout. How far you get is the measure of success.

Beginner's checklist

  • Practice early sections until the flips become automatic.
  • Watch for obstacles on the surface you are about to land on.
  • Keep calm after a close call so you do not over-flip.

Control scheme

  • Any Key – flip Gravity
  • Esc – pause

Strategy notes

The sequel rewards memory as much as reaction. Learn the courses' tricky sections and develop a reliable flip pattern for each. Spot safe landing zones in advance and aim for them. When the screen is busy, focus on the next landing spot rather than every obstacle at once.

Final verdict

A tougher, more refined version of the original that fans will appreciate. Newcomers may find the difficulty steep, and the visuals remain simple. A good next step for anyone who mastered the first game.
