Galaga Online

Inside Galaga: An unofficial Flash take on the classic alien shootout

This version of the famous arcade shooter is an unofficial Flash adaptation, credited to Robotube Studios. The setup is pure arcade history: your ship sits at the bottom of the screen, a swarm of aliens gathers above, and the only way out is to shoot them all before they get you. It is old-fashioned, and proudly so.

Gameplay overview

You move left and right and fire upward at the aliens. They arrive in formation, then peel off to swoop down at you in attack runs, firing as they come. Clearing a wave brings the next one, often faster and more aggressive. Avoiding fire and incoming attackers is as important as shooting accurately.

Beginner's checklist

  • Keep moving so swooping aliens cannot line up an easy hit.
  • Shoot aliens as they peel off, when they are closest.
  • Thin out the edges of the formation to reduce incoming attack angles.

Strategy notes

Attack runs follow patterns, so learn them. Aliens that break away tend to follow similar paths, giving you a chance to position and shoot as they come down. Avoid sitting directly under the formation, where you are exposed to several attackers at once. Steady, accurate fire beats spraying shots across the screen.

Final verdict

A faithful enough tribute to a legendary arcade game, and fun in short sessions. As an unofficial Flash version, it lacks the polish of the original. Good for a quick dose of classic shooting.

Details
