Inside Gobdun: Dungeon fights where stepping forward means swinging

Venturous built this dungeon brawler around a control scheme that sounds odd and plays cleverly. There is no separate attack button. Moving forward is attacking, so every step toward an enemy is a commitment. That single design choice turns each corridor fight into a small tactical puzzle about facing, timing and when to stand your ground.

Gameplay overview

Up carries you ahead and strikes whatever is in the way, down flips your facing and brings up a block, and left and right rotate you toward new threats. P pauses, and WASD works too. Dungeons are full of enemies approaching from different sides, so the real skill is facing the right way at the right moment.

Beginner's checklist

  • Swing round toward an attacker before it arrives, not after.
  • Raise your block when a crowd starts to close in.
  • Only press forward when you actually mean to strike.
  • Use quiet moments between fights to look in every direction.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – move Forward / Attack
  • Down Arrow – turn Around / Block
  • Left / Right Arrow Keys – turn Left / Right
  • P – pause
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Facing is everything. Since attacking and moving are the same action, positioning yourself so enemies come at you from the front lets you strike first. Use blocking to buy time when outnumbered, then turn and attack one opponent at a time. Avoid wandering forward carelessly, since each step can commit you to a fight.

Final verdict

The unusual controls give it real personality and a satisfying tactical layer. They also take some getting used to, and the dungeons can feel repetitive. A good pick for players who enjoy thoughtful, deliberate combat.
