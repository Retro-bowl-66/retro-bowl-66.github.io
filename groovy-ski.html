Inside Groovy Ski: A one-click ski run through trees and rocks

Bright colors and a jaunty name do a good job of hiding how tense this little skiing game can get. Your skier zigzags down a snowy slope, and the only control is a click that switches direction. Trees, rocks and other obstacles sit in your path. Getting through them is all about clicking at precisely the right moment.

Gameplay overview

Every click flips the skier's heading between left and right diagonals. Obstacles appear down the slope at varying intervals, so you need to time each switch to slip between them. The further you go, the faster and denser things become. Distance traveled is your score, and a collision ends the run.

Beginner's checklist

  • Click slightly before you need to turn, since the skier takes time to change direction.
  • Look further down the slope than the next obstacle.
  • Keep your clicks calm and rhythmic rather than frantic.

Control scheme

  • Click – change Direction

Strategy notes

The zigzag means you can never go straight, so every gap must be approached diagonally. Plan two or three switches ahead, picking a route through the obstacles rather than reacting to each one. When the slope gets crowded, commit to a line early. Hesitation is usually what sends you into a tree.

Final verdict

Cheerful and easy to pick up, with a good sense of rising tension. It is very simple, and runs start to feel alike. Fine for a few quick descents.
