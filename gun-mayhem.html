Gun Mayhem Online

Inside Gun Mayhem: Knock your rival off the stage with recoil and lead

Kevin Gu's Flash duelist became a staple of two-player sessions on shared keyboards, and its central twist explains why. Guns in this game do not drain health. They push. Stick figures stand on a handful of floating platforms, and every shot shoves your opponent a little closer to the edge. Fall off and you lose a life.

Gameplay overview

Matches play out on compact stages with gaps between platforms. Each weapon has its own knockback, rate of fire and recoil, and new weapons drop in during the fight. Jumping and double jumping help you recover from big hits. The last fighter with lives remaining wins. Every match is quick, so rematches come naturally.

Beginner's checklist

  • Stay near the center of the stage, away from the edges.
  • Pick up weapons that push hard, even if they fire slowly.
  • Use your double jump to recover after being blasted off a ledge.
  • Keep shooting while your opponent is airborne to push them further.

Strategy notes

Positioning is the whole fight. The player closer to the center has more room to absorb hits, so take the middle early. Recoil from your own gun can push you backward, which is dangerous near edges. Combine shots with jumps to keep an opponent in the air and off balance until they drift over the void.

Final verdict

Simple, fast and very fun with a friend, it remains a classic local multiplayer game. Playing against the computer is less interesting, and the presentation is basic. A great choice for two players sharing a keyboard.

Details
