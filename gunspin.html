Inside GunSpin: A gun that flies by firing backward

Moosite SL's 2019 game makes the weapon the hero. There is no character holding it, just a gun spinning through the air. Every shot fires a bullet and kicks the gun in the opposite direction, so recoil becomes your only means of travel. The aim is to go as far as possible while keeping the gun airborne and pointed the right way.

Gameplay overview

Clicking fires, and the recoil sends the gun spinning away from the barrel's direction. Because aiming and steering are the same action, every shot needs to be timed for the moment the barrel points away from where you want to go. Running out of ammunition brings you back to earth, so pickups along the way matter.

Beginner's checklist

  • Shoot when the barrel points down and back to gain height and distance.
  • Watch the spin so you can time shots to the right angle.
  • Collect pickups that keep you supplied with ammunition.

Control scheme

  • Click – shoot

Strategy notes

Timing the spin is the core skill. Each shot sends the gun rotating, so wait for the barrel to come around to a useful angle before firing again. Firing downward lifts you, while firing backward pushes you forward. Steady chains of well-timed shots go much further than frantic clicking.

Final verdict

Inventive and satisfying once the spin timing clicks, it turns recoil into a clever traversal mechanic. The randomness of the spin can feel frustrating, and runs become repetitive. A fun choice for players who like quirky physics games.
