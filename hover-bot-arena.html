Hover Bot Arena Online

Inside Hover Bot Arena: Hovering robots trading fire in a closed arena

Diseased Productions' 2007 Flash shooter puts you in control of a hovering combat robot and drops you into arena battles against other machines. There is no story to speak of, just a closed space, a weapon and opponents who want you scrapped. It is an early example of the arena shooter format in a browser.

Gameplay overview

You glide around the arena, aim and fire at opponents. Because the robot hovers, movement has a floaty momentum that takes some getting used to. Obstacles break up the space, and making use of them is essential when you are outgunned. Wrecking every opposing machine is the goal.

Beginner's checklist

  • Get used to the hover momentum before charging into fights.
  • Keep obstacles between you and opponents when you need a breather.
  • Circle opponents rather than approaching them head on.

Strategy notes

Motion is your best defense. A robot that keeps moving is harder to hit, so strafe while firing rather than stopping. Duck behind scenery to break line of sight when things go badly, then pop out to attack. Learn how your robot drifts so you can position precisely, since overshooting cover leaves you exposed.

Final verdict

An interesting piece of early browser shooting with a distinctive floaty feel. It is dated in presentation and limited in content. Worth a look for fans of Flash-era arena games.
