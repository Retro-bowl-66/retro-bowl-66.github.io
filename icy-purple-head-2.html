Inside Icy Purple Head 2: Switch between slippery ice and bouncy normal

Mini Duck Games' physics puzzle gives its purple hero a single, clever trick. Hold the button and the character freezes into a slippery block of ice. Let go and it returns to its soft, bouncy normal self. Each form behaves differently on slopes, ledges and gaps, and the puzzles are built around knowing which one to use and when.

Gameplay overview

Holding turns you icy, releasing turns you back. In ice form you glide across surfaces and can rest in places the soft form could not, while the normal form bounces, rolls and drops. Levels require switching at the right moments to reach the goal, often mid-motion. Timing the change is as important as choosing the form.

Beginner's checklist

  • Experiment with each form on the first levels to learn how they move.
  • Switch to ice to slide down slopes or across gaps.
  • Return to normal form when you need to bounce or stop.

Control scheme

  • Hold – turns Icy
  • Release – turns Normal

Strategy notes

Most puzzles hinge on switching mid-movement. Slide in ice form to gain speed, then switch to normal to bounce off a surface at the right angle. Watch for spots where only one form can stay put. When a level stumps you, try the opposite form at each key point.

Final verdict

Clever and charming, it gets a lot from a simple switching mechanic. Some levels need very precise timing, which can feel fiddly. A good pick for players who enjoy light physics puzzles.
