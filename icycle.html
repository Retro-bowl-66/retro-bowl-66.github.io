Inside Icycle: A dreamlike bike ride across a frozen world

Damp Gnat Games' 2017 bike game plays less like a race and more like a peculiar dream. The route crosses a frozen, unreal world whose scenery keeps changing and catching you off guard, and the tone is hushed and strange rather than competitive. Mechanically it is a balancing act, but the mood is what stays with you.

Gameplay overview

You pedal forward, keep the bike balanced and press on through the icy terrain. Obstacles and gaps require careful control, and some sections play with the strange environment in unexpected ways. There is no rival to beat, so the challenge comes from the course itself and from staying upright as the scenery grows weirder.

Beginner's checklist

  • Pedal steadily rather than in bursts to keep balance.
  • Slow down before unfamiliar sections to see what is coming.
  • Watch the scenery closely, since the environment itself sometimes becomes the obstacle.

Strategy notes

Balance and patience matter more than speed. Approach jumps and gaps with controlled momentum, and avoid sudden changes in pace. The surreal environment can be disorienting, so focus on the path immediately ahead. When a section keeps beating you, try a slower approach, because the course often rewards care.

Final verdict

Memorable, atmospheric and unlike most bike games, it offers a distinctive experience. The odd tone and slow pace will not suit everyone, and some sections are fiddly. A good pick for players who value mood over speed.
