Infiltrating the Airship Online

Inside Infiltrating the Airship: Henry boards the Toppat Clan's flying fortress

Released in 2013, this Henry Stickmin adventure sends the series' hapless hero up into the clouds. A giant airship belonging to the Toppat Clan drifts overhead, and Henry needs to get aboard. As always, you decide how, and as always, most of your choices end in ways that are spectacularly unhelpful for Henry and very funny for you.

Gameplay overview

At each key moment you click one of several options, from clever plans to obviously doomed ones. Failures play out as short animated gags before returning you to the choice. Successful choices branch into different paths, and the story has more than one ending depending on how you proceed. Seeing every outcome takes several runs.

Beginner's checklist

  • Click every option at each fork to catch all the jokes.
  • Keep a mental tally of which picks open fresh branches.
  • Play again afterward to reach the other ending.

Control scheme

  • Click – action

Strategy notes

There is no wrong way to enjoy this, only incomplete ways. Explore every branch, since failure animations hide many of the best jokes. Pay attention to how earlier choices shape later scenes. When you reach an ending, go back and take a different route through the key decision to see the other outcome.

Final verdict

Funny, varied and polished, it is a highlight of the series. Like the other entries, it offers little challenge beyond curiosity. A great choice for anyone who enjoys interactive comedy.
