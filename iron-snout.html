Inside Iron Snout: A kung fu pig against a pack of wolves

Before Bacon May Die, SnoutUp Games made this 2014 fighter starring a pig with remarkable martial arts skills and a serious wolf problem. Wolves attack from both sides of the screen, and the pig answers with kicks, punches and well-timed dodges. The humor is broad, but the combat is genuinely crisp and satisfying.

Gameplay overview

Enemies come in from left and right, and you respond with directional attacks and evasions. Each wolf type has its own approach, so reading what is coming matters as much as fast reactions. The pace climbs as waves pile up. Surviving as long as possible and racking up a score is the goal.

Beginner's checklist

  • Watch both sides of the screen, since wolves come from each direction.
  • Time attacks for when wolves are within reach, not before.
  • Use dodges against attacks you cannot answer with a strike.

Strategy notes

Rhythm and direction are everything. Respond to each threat in the order it will reach you, rather than the order it appeared. Avoid wasted strikes that leave you open. As waves intensify, look for patterns in enemy types and practice a reliable answer for each. Calm, precise inputs survive longer than mashing.

Final verdict

Quick, crunchy and funny, it is a satisfying arcade brawler. The single-screen setup limits variety, and long sessions feel similar. A great pick for short bursts of reflex combat.
