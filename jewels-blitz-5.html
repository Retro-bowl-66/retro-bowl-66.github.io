Inside Jewels Blitz 5: Swap gems, trigger cascades, repeat

Swap-three puzzles are older than most of the phones they now live on, and this HTML5 take sticks closely to the tradition. Trade places between two adjacent gems, line up a trio or better of one color and watch them pop while fresh stones drop in to fill the hole. Nothing here will surprise you, which is rather the point.

Gameplay overview

Dragging a gem onto a neighbor swaps them, and if that forms a line of three or more matching colors, the line clears. New gems fall into the gaps, which can trigger further matches automatically. Chain reactions score extra, so setting them up is the main source of big points. The aim is to keep scoring as the board refreshes.

Beginner's checklist

  • Look for matches near the bottom to trigger cascades.
  • Check for longer lines of four or five before taking a quick three.
  • Scan the whole board rather than the area you last played.

Control scheme

  • Drag – move Tiles
  • Drop – switch Tiles

Strategy notes

The best matches create more matches. Clearing gems low on the board causes everything above to shift, often producing chains for free. In many match three games longer lines create special gems, so look for them when available. When no obvious move exists, choose the swap that disturbs the most of the board.

Final verdict

Comfortable, colorful and easy to play, it is a solid example of the genre. It brings nothing new to the formula. A fine choice for a relaxed puzzle session.
