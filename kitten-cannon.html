Inside Kitten Cannon: Fire a kitten from a cannon and hope for trampolines

Dan Fleming's 2005 Flash game is one of the founding classics of the launcher genre, and its premise is gloriously silly. A cartoon kitten is loaded into a cannon and fired across a field. How far it travels depends on your aim and on what it hits on the way, from bouncy trampolines to explosive surprises and less friendly obstacles.

Gameplay overview

You set the cannon's angle and power, then fire. Once the kitten is airborne, it bounces off whatever it lands on, with some objects launching it further and others stopping it dead. Distance is your score, and the random layout of objects makes every shot a little different. Hitting the right items is part skill and part luck.

Beginner's checklist

  • Experiment with angles to find a reliable launch.
  • Learn which objects help and which end the flight.
  • Expect luck to play a big role in your longest shots.

Strategy notes

Launch angle matters most. A moderate angle with high power usually travels furthest, giving the kitten time to hit helpful objects. Watch which items bounce it onward and which stop it, and aim your landings accordingly. Since object placement varies, multiple attempts are the best way to chase a big score.

Final verdict

Simple, silly and still entertaining, it is a fun piece of Flash history. Its reliance on luck limits depth, and sessions are short. A good pick for a quick laugh and a few launches.

Details
