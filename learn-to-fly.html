Learn To Fly Online

Inside Learn To Fly: A penguin determined to prove biology wrong

Light Bringer Games' 2009 progression game stars a penguin who has heard that penguins cannot fly and refuses to accept it. Every attempt starts at the top of a ramp, ends somewhere on the snow and earns money for better equipment. Bit by bit, the stubborn bird gets further, higher and faster toward its impossible goal.

Gameplay overview

Each attempt is a slide, a launch and a fight to stay airborne. Payment arrives afterward, scaled to how far, how high and how fast you went, and it buys sturdier sleds, better gliders and eventually rockets. Across dozens of tries, those early belly flops stretch into long, graceful arcs.

Beginner's checklist

  • Spend early money on a better ramp or sled for faster launches.
  • Tilt the penguin to glide rather than plunging into the snow.
  • Use rockets in the air, not on the ramp, for maximum distance.

Strategy notes

Upgrade order matters. Launch speed and glider quality give the biggest early gains, while rockets and fuel shine later. In flight, a shallow angle keeps the penguin gliding efficiently. Fire boosts when you start losing height rather than right at launch. Each flight builds funds for the next upgrade, so steady progress beats perfect runs.

Final verdict

Charming and steadily rewarding, it is a classic of the Flash progression genre. Flights become repetitive as you grind for upgrades. A great pick for fans of launch-and-upgrade games.
