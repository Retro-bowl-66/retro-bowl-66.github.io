Little Alchemy 2 Online

Inside Little Alchemy 2: Combine air, earth, fire and water into everything

Recloak's 2017 sequel begins with the four classical elements and a simple question: what happens if you mix them? Air and fire, water and earth, then the results of those pairings with each other. Gradually your collection grows from basic materials to living things, inventions, myths and jokes. Discovery is the entire point.

Gameplay overview

Dragging one element onto another combines them, and if the pairing makes sense, a new item appears in your collection. Some combinations are logical, others playful or punny. There is no timer or score pressure, so you explore at your own pace. The goal, if you want one, is to find every item the game contains.

Beginner's checklist

  • Combine each new item with the basic four elements first.
  • Think about how things relate in real life, then try those pairings.
  • Use hints when you get stuck instead of guessing randomly for too long.

Control scheme

  • Drag – move Element
  • Drop – merge Elements

Strategy notes

Systematic play is efficient. Each time you discover something, try combining it with the four starting elements and with recent discoveries. Think in categories, such as weather, life or technology, and follow chains of logic. Some answers rely on wordplay, so when logic fails, consider puns.

Final verdict

Relaxing, clever and pleasantly addictive, it turns discovery into a satisfying loop. Some combinations feel arbitrary, and progress slows near the end. A great choice for calm, thoughtful play between lectures.
