Inside Matrix Rampage: Gunfights with a borrowed film-school swagger

Flash shooters from 2006 tended to be scrappy, and this one is no exception, but it carries itself with more style than most of its peers. The title makes no secret of which famous sci-fi film it admires. What you get is a run and gun scramble where the only reliable defense is refusing to stand still.

Gameplay overview

Each area sends waves of hostile gunmen at you, and the goal is to clear them out and survive long enough to reach the next stretch. You move, aim and fire almost constantly, because attackers arrive from different directions and rarely line up politely. The real skill lies in positioning and choosing targets in a sensible order.

Beginner's checklist

  • Keep moving between bursts, since a stationary hero is exactly what every gunman wants.
  • Shoot the closest threat first rather than the one that looks scariest.
  • Watch both edges of the play area, because reinforcements often enter from the side you just cleared.

Strategy notes

The usual trap is tunnel vision. Locking onto one enemy while two more settle in behind you is how most runs end. Clear some space first, then push into the gap you created. When a wave thins out, drift back toward the middle, where you can react to either flank, and give incoming fire a wider berth than it seems to need.

Final verdict

Matrix Rampage delivers short, punchy firefights and a pleasing sense of style for its age. The repetition shows after a few areas, and the movement feels stiff by modern standards. Fans of retro shooters will find it worth a session or two.
