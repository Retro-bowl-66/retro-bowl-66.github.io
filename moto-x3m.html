Moto X3M Online

Inside Moto X3M: Flips, explosions and a very honest stopwatch

Madpuffers released the first Moto X3M in 2015, and its formula has been borrowed endlessly since. You ride a dirt bike across short, trap-filled tracks while the clock keeps running. Crashes are constant and restarts are instant, so failure costs almost nothing except pride.

Gameplay overview

Each level is a sprint across ramps, moving platforms and explosive hazards. You throttle, brake and lean the rider to control the bike's rotation in the air and keep the wheels down on landing. Checkpoints split longer tracks into manageable pieces. Your finishing time decides how many stars you earn, and every completed flip lowers that time.

Beginner's checklist

  • Learn to land with both wheels level, because a clean landing keeps your momentum.
  • Use checkpoints as practice markers and master one section at a time.
  • Attempt flips only when a jump is long enough to finish the rotation.

Strategy notes

Three stars usually require a mix of speed and flips, but greedy flips cost more than they save when they end in a crash. Lean back slightly on ramps to keep the front wheel up, then level out before touching down. Watch for traps triggered by your arrival, such as falling objects or explosions, and adjust your pace so they go off before you get there.

Final verdict

Moto X3M is still one of the best stunt bike games in the browser, with tight handling and excellent level design. Later stages can feel punishing, and the presentation is functional rather than pretty. Anyone who likes short skill challenges should start here.
