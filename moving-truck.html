Inside Moving Truck: Delivery driving where the cargo fights back

Anyone who has carried a full box down a flight of stairs understands this game instantly. 7Spot Games hands you a truck, loads cargo onto the open back and points you at a stretch of bumpy terrain. Getting to the finish is easy. Getting there with the load still on board is the actual job.

Gameplay overview

Each level is a short haul over hills, dips and rough ground. You drive forward and back, tilt the truck to keep it level and fire the nitro when a slope demands it. Cargo shifts and bounces with every bump, and dropping it can cost you the level, so careful driving is rewarded far more than raw speed.

Beginner's checklist

  • Drive slowly over bumps, because cargo flies off when the truck jolts.
  • Tilt the truck back on descents and forward on climbs to keep the load level.
  • Restart early if a crate falls off instead of finishing a doomed run.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – forward
  • Down Arrow – backward
  • Left / Right Arrow – tilt
  • Space – nitro
  • R – restart
  • Esc – pause
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Smooth inputs matter more than anything else. Sudden acceleration or braking jerks the cargo around, so press and release gradually. Before a big drop, slow down and tilt the truck so the bed stays level as you land. Nitro is powerful but blunt; one burst at the base of a steep hill beats holding it across a whole section.

Final verdict

Moving Truck is a cleverly frustrating physics game that turns a mundane job into a tense balancing act. The physics can feel random, and the levels follow a similar pattern. Players who enjoy careful driving puzzles will find it rewarding.
