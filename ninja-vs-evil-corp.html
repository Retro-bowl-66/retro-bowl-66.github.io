Ninja vs Evil Corp Online

Inside Ninja vs Evil Corp: One ninja against one very evil company

Corporate villainy is an evergreen target, and Remil Vansteelandt's 2020 platformer sends a lone ninja to deal with it. The setup is minimal: a sinister company, levels full of traps and enemies, and a hero who has to get through all of them. What follows is a tidy, timing-focused platformer with an old-school feel.

Gameplay overview

You run, jump and fight through levels set inside the corporation's facilities. Hazards and enemies are positioned to test your timing, and each new stage introduces fresh obstacles or tougher combinations of familiar ones. Mistakes send you back to retry, so success comes from learning layouts and executing jumps precisely.

Beginner's checklist

  • Learn how far and how high the ninja jumps before taking risks.
  • Watch enemy patterns for a moment before moving past them.
  • Retry sections calmly, because rushing usually repeats the same mistake.

Strategy notes

Platformers like this reward observation. Pause briefly before each new section to see how hazards and enemies move, then commit to a plan. Hesitating halfway through a jump sequence is often worse than a quick, decisive run. When fighting, pick spots where no hazard is also threatening you, so you only manage one danger at a time.

Final verdict

Ninja vs Evil Corp is a solid, well-paced platformer with a fun premise. It does not do much that is new, and the difficulty can feel uneven. Platformer fans looking for a tidy challenge will be content.
