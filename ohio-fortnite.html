Ohio Fortnite Online

Inside Ohio Fortnite: A meme-titled shooter with standard bones

Two internet jokes went into the title, and very little of the famous battle royale it name-checks made it into the actual game. DiaoChangNan's Unity shooter is an unofficial project with its own design and no connection to that commercial title. Strip away the meme branding and you find a conventional shooter with a surprisingly full set of controls.

Gameplay overview

You move around the environment, aim with the mouse and fight enemies using several weapons you can swap between. Ammunition matters, because reloading leaves you exposed at the worst moments, and a map helps you keep your bearings in larger areas. Sprinting and jumping help you reposition mid-fight. The goal is simply to survive encounters and outgun whoever you meet.

Beginner's checklist

  • Check the map early so you know the layout before the shooting starts.
  • Reload behind cover rather than out in the open.
  • Try each weapon to learn which suits close fights and which suits distance.

Control scheme

  • WASD Keys – move
  • Mouse – aim
  • Click – shoot
  • Space – jump
  • Shift – run
  • R – reload
  • F – action
  • M – map
  • Number Keys – switch Weapons
  • Esc – pause

Strategy notes

Positioning decides most fights. Move from cover to cover, peek briefly to shoot and pull back to reload. Track your ammunition so you are never caught empty during an engagement. Use the sprint key to escape bad situations, and jump only when it actually helps, since a jump commits you to a predictable arc that is easy to hit.

Final verdict

Ohio Fortnite delivers serviceable shooting with plenty of controls and a jokey name. It feels generic and unpolished, and the title promises more personality than the game delivers. Casual shooter fans may enjoy a few rounds.
