Pacman Online

Inside Pacman: Dots, ghosts and a maze that never changes

Some designs are so tight that four decades of imitators have barely improved them. This unofficial browser version of the maze classic keeps the essentials: a yellow hero, corridors lined with dots and four ghosts who would very much like to catch you. Clear the maze, stay alive and try not to get greedy.

Gameplay overview

You steer through the maze eating every dot to finish the level, while ghosts roam the corridors hunting you. Touching one costs a life. Large power pellets briefly turn the tables, letting you chase and eat the ghosts for bonus points. Once the dots are gone, a new round begins, typically a little faster and harder than the last.

Beginner's checklist

  • Clear areas far from the ghosts first and save risky corridors for later.
  • Hold off on power pellets until ghosts are close enough to catch.
  • Use the side tunnels to shake off pursuers.
  • Pause whenever you need a breather instead of playing distracted.

Control scheme

  • Arrow Keys – move
  • P – pause

Strategy notes

Pac-Man rewards route planning. Learn a path that clears the maze without much doubling back. In the arcade original, each ghost has its own personality, one chasing directly while others try to cut you off, so watch how these ones move and adjust. Eat ghosts in quick succession after a power pellet, because each one in the chain is worth more.

Final verdict

This version captures what made the original great, with simple controls and a perfectly balanced loop. As an unofficial port, it may lack some polish and arcade details. Anyone who enjoys classic arcade games will find it a reliable pick.
