Inside Papa's Donuteria: Shaping, frying and filling, one donut at a time

Dough cutters, a deep fryer and a row of filling nozzles make up the workspace in Papa's Donuteria, Flipline's June 2014 entry. Customers ask for specific shapes, fillings and toppings, and every donut has to travel through each station before it is ready. It is one of the most hands-on games in the series, with plenty of little skills to master.

Gameplay overview

You cut each donut from dough in the requested shape, fry it until both sides are golden, inject any fillings and then decorate with icing, drizzles and toppings. Each stage is judged, along with how long customers wait. Tips pay for shop upgrades, while new ranks introduce extra ingredients and pickier customers who expect even more precision.

Beginner's checklist

  • Turn donuts in the fryer at the right moment for even color.
  • Fill donuts carefully, since overfilling and underfilling both cost points.
  • Fry new donuts while finishing older ones at the decorating station.

Strategy notes

Frying is the trickiest step, so watch each donut closely rather than trusting rough guesses. Use the time donuts spend in the oil to decorate finished ones. Keep the ticket in view while decorating, since icing and drizzles must match exactly. Spend early tips on anything that saves time at the fryer.

Final verdict

Donuteria is a busy, detailed entry with satisfying hands-on stations. The number of steps can feel overwhelming at first, and the formula is familiar. Players who enjoy careful, multi-step work will find plenty to like.
