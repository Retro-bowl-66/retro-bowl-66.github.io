Paper Minecraft Online

Inside Paper Minecraft: A Scratch project that punches far above its weight

Griffpatch is something of a legend on Scratch, and Paper Minecraft, first shared in December 2014, is a big reason why. It rebuilds the famous block game as a two dimensional fan project, with mining, crafting and building squeezed into a platform designed for learning to code. Mojang owns the real thing; this is a tribute.

Gameplay overview

You explore a side-view world made of blocks, mining resources, crafting tools and building shelters. Survival basics carry over, with hostile mobs to avoid and materials to gather as you dig deeper. The goals are your own: build a home, hunt for rare ores or just explore. Given its Scratch origins, the amount of detail is genuinely impressive.

Beginner's checklist

  • Collect wood first to start crafting basic tools.
  • Get a roof over your head before nightfall when playing in survival.
  • Check the crafting menu often to see what you can now build.

Strategy notes

Treat Paper Minecraft like its 3D inspiration, but use the side view to your advantage. You can see caves and resources clearly, which makes planning digs easy. Keep a stockpile of building materials and tools. Because it runs on Scratch, performance can dip in complex worlds, so scale back huge builds if things start to slow down.

Final verdict

Paper Minecraft is an impressive fan project and a fine 2D take on block building. It is limited by its platform, with simpler mechanics and occasional slowdowns. Fans of the original and Scratch enthusiasts will enjoy it.
