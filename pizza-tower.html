Pizza Tower Online

Inside Pizza Tower: A frantic chef, a tower of levels and no brakes

Peppino Spaghetti has a pizzeria to save and very little patience for walking. Pizza Tower, credited to Tour De Pizza and listed for January 2023, is a breakneck platformer with a loud, hand-drawn cartoon style inspired by nineties handheld games. What runs in the browser here is an unofficial build, but the core idea comes through: speed, momentum and chaos.

Gameplay overview

You charge through levels at high speed, smashing enemies and obstacles while keeping a combo meter alive. Each level ends with a frantic escape back to the start against the clock, and a rank at the end grades how well you did. Secrets and collectibles reward exploration, so every level invites a second, faster run.

Beginner's checklist

  • Hold the run button to build speed, and keep it whenever possible.
  • Keep the combo meter going by hitting enemies in quick succession.
  • Learn each level's layout before worrying about the escape sequence.

Strategy notes

Momentum is your most valuable resource. Breaking stride costs time and combo, so learn where the level wants you to run, slide or bounce. During the escape, routes can differ from the way in, so practice them. Ranks reward speed and combos, so once a level is familiar, replay it aiming for a cleaner, unbroken run.

Final verdict

Pizza Tower is a wildly energetic platformer with outstanding style and movement. This unofficial browser version may not match the original's polish, and the pace can overwhelm newcomers. Fans of fast platformers should try it.
