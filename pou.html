Pou Online

Inside Pou: A small brown blob that needs constant attention

Virtual pets ask for very little skill and quite a lot of attention. Pou, credited to Zakeh Limited with a 2021 date, is a browser take on the famous pet app, and this version is unofficial. You look after a small, round creature, feeding it, cleaning it and keeping it rested, and in return you get the satisfaction of a contented pet.

Gameplay overview

Your pet has needs that drop over time, such as hunger, energy and fun. You feed it, wash it, put it to bed and play mini games to raise its mood. Coins earned from those games buy food, outfits and decorations. There is no real ending; the goal is to keep your pet healthy and happy for as long as you play.

Beginner's checklist

  • Feed and clean your pet regularly to keep its stats healthy.
  • Play mini games to earn coins for food and items.
  • Put your pet to sleep whenever its energy runs low.

Strategy notes

Routine is the key. Check in regularly so needs never fall too low, and keep a stock of food bought with coins from mini games. Some mini games pay better than others, so find the ones you enjoy and are good at. Cosmetic extras can wait until the basics are covered.

Final verdict

Pou is a gentle, relaxing pet game with simple routines and charming mini games. Its loop is repetitive, and this unofficial version may lack features of the original. Younger players and fans of virtual pets will enjoy it.
