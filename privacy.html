Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Retro-Bowl-66 (retro-bowl-66.github.io) collects when you visit, how it is used and what choices you have. The short version: there are no accounts, we never ask for your name, and we do not sell personal data.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools record standard technical data when a page loads. This can include your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, the page that referred you, the pages you view and the time of your visit. We use it to keep the site running, see which games and guides get used, and find technical problems.

Advertising through Google AdSense

The site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, Google among them, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalized ads at www.aboutads.info/choices. Google describes how it uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. You can accept, refuse or change your choice later. Without consent, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads, which rely on less data.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand visits in aggregate, such as how many people opened a game page and which devices they used. It relies on cookies and similar identifiers. You can stop your visits being measured by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Games from third parties

The games are made and hosted by third parties and load inside an embedded frame. A game may set its own cookies or use local storage, for example to save progress or settings. That data is controlled by the game's developer, not by us, and their own privacy terms apply.

Children

Retro-Bowl-66 is a general audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, objection to certain processing and withdrawal of consent. California residents may opt out of the sale of personal information, but there is nothing to opt out of here: we do not sell personal data. To make a request, email us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as it helps us understand how the site is used, within the retention settings of the tools involved. Emails you send are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Updates and contact

We may revise this policy when our tools or the law change, and the date at the top will show the latest version. Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@retro-bowl-66.github.io.
