Push The Square Online

Inside Push The Square: A square, a click and a growing number

A plain square sits in the middle of the screen, and you push it. That is Push The Square, a Unity WebGL clicker by Rual Moreno Tamayo from March 2019. Each push earns points, and points buy upgrades that make pushing more lucrative. It is the clicker formula stripped to its bones, for better and worse.

Gameplay overview

Clicking pushes the square and earns points. You spend those points on upgrades, some increasing what each click earns and others adding automatic income. As upgrades stack, your numbers grow faster and the game shifts from active clicking to planning purchases. There is no story or end goal beyond watching your totals climb.

Beginner's checklist

  • Click actively early, before automatic income takes over.
  • Compare the cost and benefit of each upgrade before buying.
  • Invest in automatic income once clicking upgrades get expensive.

Strategy notes

Efficiency drives progress. Estimate roughly how much each upgrade adds per point spent and prioritize the best value. Early click upgrades help you start, but automatic income soon becomes the main source. Avoid saving too long for expensive upgrades if cheaper ones offer better returns right now.

Final verdict

Push The Square is a minimalist clicker with the genre's usual satisfaction. It has almost no depth or variety, and it wears thin quickly. Clicker fans may enjoy it briefly, while everyone else can safely skip it.
