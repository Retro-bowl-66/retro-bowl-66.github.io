Rabbit Samurai 2 Online

Inside Rabbit Samurai 2: The rabbit returns with sharper puzzles

The sword-swinging rabbit is back, and Maksim Eliseenko's sequel picks up right where the first game left off. Rabbit Samurai 2 adds new levels and challenges built around physics, keeping the cutting puzzles that made the original charming. Returning players will feel at home, while the puzzles push a little harder.

Gameplay overview

Each stage hangs its pieces from ropes, stacks them on ledges or wedges them in awkward spots, and your blade decides what drops first. Gravity and momentum handle the rest, so one stroke can set off a long chain reaction. The trick is finding the right order of actions, and the sequel tends to ask for longer, more delicate chains than before.

Beginner's checklist

  • Play the first game before this one if you can.
  • Look for chain reactions that solve several problems at once.
  • Cut in the right order, because timing often decides success.

Strategy notes

Planning pays off even more than in the original. Before cutting, imagine the whole chain of events, from the first falling object to the last. When a puzzle seems impossible, look for an object you have not used yet. Small timing differences can change the outcome, so try slight variations before abandoning a plan.

Final verdict

Rabbit Samurai 2 is a solid sequel that builds sensibly on a clever idea. It does not change much, and some levels lean on trial and error. Fans of the first game will enjoy the new puzzles.
