Inside Rabbit Samurai: A sword-wielding rabbit with physics on its side

Swords are rarely puzzle tools, but in Maksim Eliseenko's Rabbit Samurai the blade is the key to every level. A samurai rabbit faces a small physics puzzle each stage, and a well-placed cut sets off a chain of falling, rolling and swinging objects. The HTML5 game keeps things short and tidy, with each level a self-contained problem.

Gameplay overview

You study the layout and decide where to swing or cut, then watch the physics play out as objects react to gravity and momentum. One cut is sometimes enough; elsewhere you need a sequence, timed carefully. The goal is to complete each level's objective with clever cuts rather than brute force, moving on to harder puzzles as you go.

Beginner's checklist

  • Look over the whole level before cutting anything.
  • Predict where objects will fall after each cut.
  • Restart and experiment whenever a plan does not work.

Strategy notes

Think about cause and effect. Each cut releases objects that move according to physics, so trace their likely paths before acting. The order of cuts often matters, especially when one falling object must arrive before another. Experimentation is part of the design, so failed attempts teach you how the level works.

Final verdict

Rabbit Samurai is a charming, clever physics puzzler with a fun premise. Its levels are short and the challenge is moderate. Fans of cutting and physics puzzles will enjoy it.
