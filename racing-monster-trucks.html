Racing Monster Trucks Online

Inside Racing Monster Trucks: Tall trucks, rough ground and a race clock

Tall trucks and rough terrain make an awkward combination, and Racing Monster Trucks leans into that awkwardness. This HTML5 racing game puts you behind the wheel of a towering truck and asks you to reach the finish line as quickly as possible. The bumps, hills and ramps between you and the end are the real opponents.

Gameplay overview

Throttle and brake carry you forward while you keep the tall body balanced over every rise and fall. Because the truck sits so high, it tips easily if you push too hard over a crest or land badly off a ramp. Each track is a race against time, and reaching the finish quickly without flipping is the goal.

Beginner's checklist

  • Ease off before big jumps to avoid flipping over.
  • Keep the truck level as it lands after each ramp.
  • Learn each track's layout to find faster lines.

Strategy notes

Speed and balance pull in opposite directions. Too much throttle over bumps tips the truck, while too little costs time. Find the speed at which the truck stays stable and push harder only on flat stretches. On landings, aim to touch down evenly. Replaying tracks teaches you where to accelerate and where to hold back.

Final verdict

Racing Monster Trucks is a simple, enjoyable physics racer with satisfying handling. It lacks variety and depth, and tracks can feel similar. Casual racing fans will find it a pleasant way to pass ten minutes.
