Riddle School 2 Online

Inside Riddle School 2: Back at the same strange school

Six months after the first game, JonBro brought Phil back for Riddle School 2 in November 2006. The setting is familiar and so is the humor, with new puzzles and characters filling the halls. Sequels to short comedy games can feel thin, but this one adds enough new jokes to justify the return trip.

Gameplay overview

You explore the school again, collecting odd items and using them to solve puzzles that lead toward escape. The logic remains playful and often surprising, so trying different combinations is part of the experience. New characters bring their own requests and gags. The adventure is still brief, but it is a step up in variety from the original.

Beginner's checklist

  • Explore every room before you start using items.
  • Click on characters, as they often hint at solutions.
  • Experiment with unusual combinations whenever you get stuck.

Strategy notes

The trick is to stop thinking realistically. Many solutions rely on visual gags or wordplay, so look closely at objects and consider how they might be used in silly ways. When stuck, go back through the rooms, since a new item may unlock new options. Patience and curiosity solve nearly everything.

Final verdict

Riddle School 2 is a fun, slightly more varied follow-up with the same charm. It is still short and simple. Players who enjoyed the first game will happily return.
