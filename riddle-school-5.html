Riddle School 5 Online

Inside Riddle School 5: The day-after sequel that wraps things up

One day after Riddle School 4, JonBro released Riddle School 5 on April 2, 2010, so the two play almost like a single story split in half. It is another chapter of the comic escape series, with jokes doing as much work as logic, and it is the last numbered entry in the main run.

Gameplay overview

Exploring, grabbing whatever is not nailed down and solving puzzles where the punchline is usually the answer make up the whole loop. Straightforward thinking rarely helps, so expect to try ideas that sound ridiculous. The game builds on everything the series has done, with familiar characters and fresh challenges. The aim, once again, is to escape your predicament with wit and curiosity.

Beginner's checklist

  • Play Riddle School 4 first, since the two were released a day apart and read best together.
  • Click on everything to find hidden items and gags.
  • Think creatively whenever an item seems useless, because it almost never is.

Strategy notes

By the fifth game, you will recognize the series' style, so put that knowledge to work. Expect solutions to involve visual jokes, odd combinations and playful twists. Explore thoroughly, revisit areas with new items and try everything on everything. The game rewards patience and a willingness to try silly ideas.

Final verdict

Riddle School 5 is a satisfying chapter that rewards long-time fans. Its humor and simple art may not suit everyone, and some solutions feel random. Players who enjoyed the earlier games should finish the story here.
