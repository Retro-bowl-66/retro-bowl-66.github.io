Riddle School Transfer 1 Online

Inside Riddle School Transfer 1: A new school, the same gloriously silly logic

Changing schools is supposed to mean a fresh start. JonBro's June 2011 spin-off moves the comic escape series to an entirely new building, but anyone hoping the jokes would grow up will be relieved to hear they have not. Odd characters, absurd puzzles and very little respect for real-world physics all made the transfer intact.

Gameplay overview

Each scene is a room to poke at. You click on objects, gather anything that can be carried and try your inventory on whatever looks promising, slowly opening a path through the new school. Puzzles run on cartoon reasoning, so the answer is often the funniest option rather than the most sensible one. Escaping is the goal, as it always has been.

Beginner's checklist

  • Get to know the new layout before trying to solve anything.
  • Pick up every portable object, however pointless it looks.
  • Click characters more than once, since repeat conversations sometimes reveal extra hints.

Strategy notes

The new setting resets your mental map, so explore broadly before narrowing down. Note which rooms have locked doors or unexplained objects; those are your open puzzles. Veterans of the numbered games should trust their instincts about silly solutions, while newcomers can treat this as a fine starting point, since it stands reasonably well by itself.

Final verdict

Transfer 1 refreshes the series with a new setting while keeping its comic heart. The puzzles remain short and occasionally arbitrary, and the art is still basic. Fans of the originals will enjoy the change of scenery.
