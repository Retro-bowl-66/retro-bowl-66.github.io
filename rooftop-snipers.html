Inside Rooftop Snipers: Wobbly gunfights where recoil is the real enemy

Two floppy marksmen stand on a rooftop, each armed and each very bad at staying upright. New Eich Games built Rooftop Snipers around that comedy. Every match is a duel to knock your rival off the building, and the controls are so minimal that your own clumsiness becomes the main opponent.

Gameplay overview

Each player has just two buttons: one to jump and one to shoot. Firing pushes your character backward with recoil, so every shot risks shoving you toward the edge. Hit your opponent and they stagger; push them off the roof and you win the round. Matches are short, and the first to win enough rounds takes the duel.

Beginner's checklist

  • Jump to dodge shots, but avoid landing too close to the edge.
  • Account for recoil before firing, especially near the drop.
  • Shoot when your opponent is airborne and harder to stabilize.

Strategy notes

Positioning beats aim. Keep yourself near the center so recoil cannot push you off, and fire when your rival is close to the edge. Jumping is both defense and setup, since a well-timed hop can dodge a shot and line up your own. Against a friend, unpredictable timing matters, so vary when you jump and shoot.

Final verdict

Rooftop Snipers is a hilarious, chaotic duel game that shines with two players. Solo play is less memorable, and the simplicity limits long-term depth. Perfect for quick laughs with a friend sharing the keyboard.
