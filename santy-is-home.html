Santy is Home Online

Inside Santy is Home: A festive visitor you really do not want

Holiday cheer gets a dark twist in Kulu's Santy is Home, a Unity WebGL horror game. A familiar festive figure has appeared where he is not welcome, and your goal is to avoid him. It is an atmospheric, slow-burn experience that leans on sound and tension rather than constant scares.

Gameplay overview

You explore a house carefully, listening for sounds and avoiding the threat that roams within it. Moving slowly and thinking ahead are essential, since rushing can draw unwanted attention. Tension builds as you explore further, and the goal is to survive and find a way to escape or complete your objectives without being caught.

Beginner's checklist

  • Play with sound on, because noises warn you of danger.
  • Move slowly and stop every few steps to listen for movement.
  • Know where you can hide before exploring further.

Strategy notes

Horror games like this reward caution. Learn the layout gradually, noting safe spots and escape routes. Listen constantly, since footsteps and other sounds reveal where the threat is. If you are spotted, break line of sight quickly. Patience is your greatest asset; most failures come from moving too fast.

Final verdict

Santy is Home is a creepy, atmospheric horror game with an amusing holiday premise. It is short and may lack depth for horror veterans. Players who enjoy tense, seasonal scares will appreciate it.
