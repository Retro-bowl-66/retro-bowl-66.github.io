Save the Doge Online

Inside Save the Doge: One line of ink between a dog and the bees

A dog, a hive and a pen: Save the Doge sets up its puzzle in seconds. Wonder Group's July 2022 game hands you a limited supply of ink and a very nervous dog, then releases a swarm. Whatever you sketched before the bees arrive is all the protection that dog gets.

Gameplay overview

Each stage shows the pup, the hive and some terrain. You sketch a barrier with the mouse, lift the pen, and physics takes over: your drawing drops, tips or wedges itself against the scenery. Then the bees pour out and hammer at it until the countdown ends. Survive the timer and the level is cleared, and frugal sketches tend to score better.

Beginner's checklist

  • Check which direction the hive faces before putting pen to screen.
  • Draw closed shapes when unsure, since gaps are where bees get through.
  • Lean drawings against walls or ground so they do not topple over.

Strategy notes

Think like an engineer rather than an artist. A thick dome looks protective but can roll away, while a simple wedge braced against a ledge often holds perfectly. Bees push and swarm, so thin lines with loose ends are risky. If a design fails, change one thing at a time. A level or two also makes a decent micro-break between assignments.

Final verdict

Save the Doge is a charming, quick puzzle game with a clever premise. Some levels feel trivial while others are oddly strict. A nice pick for short sessions and casual puzzle fans.
