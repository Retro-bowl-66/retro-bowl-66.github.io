Slime Rush TD Online

Inside Slime Rush TD: Squishy invaders and a road worth guarding

Gelatinous enemies have a long history in games, usually as the weakest thing on the screen. kiz10's Slime Rush TD flips that by sending them in growing crowds along a winding route. It is a classic tower defense in HTML5 form, bright and approachable, and it rarely tries to surprise anyone.

Gameplay overview

The slimes follow a fixed road across the map, and anything that reaches the far end costs you. You spend coins on defensive units placed along the roadside, and each defeated slime pays a little more into your budget. Blobs vary in bulk and pace, so one kind of tower rarely copes with everything. Later waves arrive thicker and tougher.

Beginner's checklist

  • Put your first towers where the road bends, so they cover two stretches at once.
  • Pair heavy hitters with fast-firing units to handle both big and quick slimes.
  • Upgrade a strong position before scattering cheap towers around the map.

Strategy notes

Coverage is the hidden stat. A tower at a corner touches far more of the route than one beside a straight, so those spots are worth claiming early. Watch which slimes are leaking through and buy the counter rather than more of the same. Upgrades tend to give better value than extra low-level towers once the waves grow.

Final verdict

Slime Rush TD is a cheerful, approachable tower defense game. It offers little that is new and may feel easy for genre veterans. Newcomers to tower defense will find it a gentle introduction.
