Smoking Barrels Online

Inside Smoking Barrels: Quick draws in a Flash-era frontier town

Every Western eventually arrives at the same scene: outlaws popping up behind barrels and windows, and one steady hand on the trigger. GmaeSheep's Smoking Barrels, from November 2007, turns that scene into a compact Flash shooting gallery. There is no story to speak of, just targets, a revolver and a reaction test.

Gameplay overview

Bandits appear in doorways, on rooftops and behind cover, and you point the mouse and click to drop them before they get a shot off. Wasted bullets hurt, because every miss costs time you do not have. Stages grow busier and faster, and clearing them depends on picking targets quickly without spraying shots around.

Beginner's checklist

  • Rest the cursor near the middle of the screen between targets.
  • Take a fraction of a second to aim instead of firing the moment something moves.
  • Learn where outlaws tend to appear in each stage and pre-aim there.

Strategy notes

Steady hands beat fast ones. A shot that lands is worth three that do not, so make each click count. Remembering the spawn spots in a stage lets you move the cursor before the bandit appears, which is the closest thing to cheating the game allows. When several enemies show up at once, deal with the nearest threat first.

Final verdict

Smoking Barrels is a fun, quick shooting gallery with a likeable Western theme. It is short and simple, and the visuals are dated. Fans of retro shooting games will enjoy it.
