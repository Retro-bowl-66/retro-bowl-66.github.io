Space Company Online

Inside Space Company: From a mining outpost to an interstellar business

Sparticle999's Space Company begins with a handful of raw resources and a modest goal: produce more of them. From there, it grows into a sprawling incremental game in which a small operation turns into a corporation reaching across the solar system and beyond. It rewards planning and patience over frantic clicking.

Gameplay overview

You gather basic materials by hand, then build machines that produce them automatically. Many machines need energy, so power generation becomes an early bottleneck. Research unlocks new buildings, resources and eventually space travel, which opens fresh locations and production chains. Progress is a long climb through increasingly complex systems, each layer building on the last.

Beginner's checklist

  • Build automatic producers early instead of gathering by hand for long.
  • Keep energy production ahead of demand so machines never stall.
  • Read research descriptions to see which unlocks open new paths.

Strategy notes

Bottlenecks drive every decision. When progress slows, work out which resource is holding everything back, often energy or a specific material, and invest there. Avoid overbuilding machines that drain power you cannot supply. Research is your main lever for long-term growth, so keep it moving even when short-term gains look tempting.

Final verdict

Space Company is a deep, satisfying incremental game with a strong sense of progression. Its interface is dense and the pace slow, which will frustrate impatient players. Fans of long-term management games will find plenty to chew on.
