Stealing The Diamond Online

Inside Stealing The Diamond: Henry's museum heist, told through bad decisions

Henry Stickmin is not a master thief. He is a master of improvisation, usually the disastrous kind. PuffballsUnited's July 2011 game, the third in the series, sends Henry after a priceless diamond on display in a museum. At every turn you pick how he proceeds, and the joke is that most options go hilariously wrong.

Gameplay overview

The game moves through a series of decision points. At each, you choose from several options, and the scene plays out according to your pick. Some choices advance Henry toward the diamond, while others trigger fail screens full of slapstick and pop culture references. Finding the right path is the goal, but seeing every failure is half the fun.

Beginner's checklist

  • Pick the silliest options sometimes; the fail scenes are a big part of the appeal.
  • Remember which choices worked so you can return quickly after a failure.
  • Explore every option at each step to see all the jokes.

Strategy notes

There is no real penalty for failure, so experiment freely. Each decision point usually has one or two options that advance the story, and the rest are jokes. If you want to finish efficiently, look for choices that seem sensible within the game's cartoon logic. If you want to see everything, go back and try every option at least once.

Final verdict

Stealing The Diamond is a hilarious, inventive choose-your-path comedy and a highlight of the Henry Stickmin series. It is short and has little challenge. Fans of absurd humor will love it.
