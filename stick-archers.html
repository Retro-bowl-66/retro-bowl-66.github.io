Inside Stick Archers: Bowstrings, arcs and very tense standoffs

Archery duels have a lovely simplicity: two figures, two bows and a lot of air between them. RHM Interactive's January 2022 game reduces the concept to stick figures firing arrows across the screen. The idea is trivial, but judging distance and arc under pressure makes every round surprisingly nervy.

Gameplay overview

You draw your bow, adjust angle and power, then release. Arrows fly in an arc affected by gravity, so you must judge the trajectory needed to hit your opponent. They shoot back, and whoever lands hits first wins the round. Small adjustments between shots matter, because a miss tells you roughly how much to correct.

Beginner's checklist

  • Use your first shot as a range finder and adjust from there.
  • Change one variable at a time, angle or power, after a miss.
  • Aim for the head when you have the range, for bigger damage.

Strategy notes

Calibration is the core skill. After each shot, note where the arrow landed relative to your target and correct gradually. Higher arcs are harder for opponents to read but take longer to land. Under pressure, avoid rushing your release, since a careful shot beats a fast miss. Against a friend, varying your timing can unsettle them.

Final verdict

Stick Archers is a tense, satisfying duel game with an elegant core idea. It lacks variety and depth over long sessions. Perfect for quick matches with a friend or a few rounds against the computer.
