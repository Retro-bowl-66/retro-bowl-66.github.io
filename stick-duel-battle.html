Stick Duel Battle Online

Inside Stick Duel Battle: Two stick figures, quick reflexes and short fights

Stripped of armor and capes, stick figures make perfectly readable duelists, and RHM Interactive leans on that. Stick Duel Battle drops two of them into a small arena with guns and lets them settle things in a hurry. There is almost nothing to learn before your first round, which is the point; the subtlety arrives later.

Gameplay overview

Each round is a brief firefight across a little stage of platforms and obstacles. You hop between ledges, line up shots and try to tag your rival before they tag you. Higher ground and solid cover both help, while standing in the open invites a quick end. Rounds tick by fast, and whoever wins enough of them takes the match.

Beginner's checklist

  • Grab high ground or cover before you start trading shots.
  • Keep shifting position so your rival never gets a still target.
  • Hold fire until you have a clear line instead of spraying blindly.

Strategy notes

Distance is the quiet deciding factor. Too close and you are trivially easy to hit; too far and your shots drift wide. Find the range where you are comfortable and work to stay there. Break line of sight with platforms, then reappear somewhere unexpected. Against a person, a little patience tends to beat constant aggression.

Final verdict

Stick Duel Battle is a quick, fun duel game with easy controls and tight rounds. It lacks depth and variety for long sessions. A good pick for short competitive bursts with a friend.
