Inside Stickman Boost: Speed bursts and the art of timing them

Boosts are only useful if you fire them at the right moment, and Stickman Boost builds an entire platformer around that idea. A stick figure crosses levels using bursts of speed to clear gaps and dodge enemies. The HTML5 game is quick to learn, but using your boost wisely turns out to be a real skill.

Gameplay overview

You run, jump and boost through levels filled with hazards. Boosting at the right moment lets you cross wide gaps, outrun threats or slip past enemies. Mistimed boosts can send you into danger or leave you short of a ledge. The goal is to reach the end of each level, with later stages demanding more precise timing.

Beginner's checklist

  • Use the boost deliberately rather than spamming it.
  • Learn how far a boost carries you before relying on it.
  • Watch enemies and time boosts to slip past them.

Strategy notes

Timing beats raw speed. Before boosting, judge the distance you need to cover and make sure your landing spot is safe. Some sections are easier at a steady pace, saving the boost for where it truly helps. When a level feels impossible, try boosting earlier or later; small adjustments can make all the difference.

Final verdict

Stickman Boost is a tidy, enjoyable platformer with a clear central idea. It is short and lacks variety. Players who enjoy timing-based platforming will find it satisfying.
