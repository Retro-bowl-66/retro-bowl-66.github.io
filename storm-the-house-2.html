Storm The House 2 Online

Inside Storm The House 2: Defending a house from waves of attackers

Ivory's March 2007 Flash game has one of the most honest titles around: enemies storm the house, and you stop them. Armed at first with a basic gun, you hold a firing line as wave after wave marches toward your walls. Between assaults, the money you earn goes on repairs, heavier weapons and hired gunmen.

Gameplay overview

Each day brings a fresh assault on the house, and you shoot attackers down before they batter through. When the shooting stops, the earnings come out: patch the walls, buy better guns or hire gunmen who add their own firepower from the windows. Different attackers demand different answers, so the shopping list changes as the days go by.

Beginner's checklist

  • Keep the walls patched between days, since a weak house collapses fast.
  • Hire extra gunmen early so someone keeps shooting while you reload.
  • Pick off attackers already hammering the walls before the newcomers.

Strategy notes

Spending is the real puzzle. Guns raise your damage, repairs buy time and hired shooters multiply your output, and starving any one of them causes trouble a few days later. Helpers compound over time, so investing in them before the assaults swell pays off. Shoot whatever is closest to breaching rather than whatever just appeared.

Final verdict

Storm The House 2 is a classic, satisfying defense game with a rewarding upgrade loop. Its visuals are dated and the action repetitive. Fans of retro Flash defense games will enjoy it.
