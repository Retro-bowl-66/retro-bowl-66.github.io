Inside Synesthesia: A platformer that paints itself as you move

Synesthesia names a real phenomenon in which one sense triggers another, such as hearing colors or tasting shapes. Breather's HTML5 platformer borrows the idea as an artistic theme. The world shifts and reacts as you travel through it, so the experience sits somewhere between a standard platformer and a small interactive art piece.

Gameplay overview

You hop across ledges, steer clear of hazards and keep heading onward, while the scenery answers each step and jump with visual changes. The platforming itself is fairly gentle, leaving room to enjoy the mood rather than sweat over tricky timing. Reaching the end of each stage is the goal, but noticing how the world responds is the real point.

Beginner's checklist

  • Move slowly at first and watch how the screen reacts to each action.
  • Try different movements to discover what changes the environment.
  • Play with headphones on if the game includes sound.

Strategy notes

Treat it as something to experience rather than beat. Wander, pause and see what your movements set off. When a jump gets awkward, the visual feedback can help you judge timing. Rushing through misses most of what the game has to offer, so give each stage a little patience.

Final verdict

Synesthesia is an intriguing, atmospheric platformer with a distinctive artistic approach. It is light on challenge and may feel slight to players seeking depth. Fans of experimental indie games will appreciate it.
