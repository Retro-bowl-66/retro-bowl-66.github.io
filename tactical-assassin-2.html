Inside Tactical Assassin 2: A sniper's patience and a single clean shot

Sniper games are about waiting, and Simon Hason Games' Tactical Assassin 2, listed with a 2005 date, is built entirely around that. Each mission hands you a rifle, a briefing and a view across a street, rooftop or courtyard. Somewhere in that scene is the person you have been hired to remove, and nobody else should be harmed.

Gameplay overview

Missions begin with instructions describing the target, then switch to the scope. You scan the area, match the description to a figure and wait for an unobstructed moment. Shooting a bystander or the wrong suspect is usually a quick route to failure. Later missions crowd the scene and keep targets moving, so identifying them becomes as demanding as aiming.

Beginner's checklist

  • Read the mission briefing closely to pin down your target.
  • Wait for a clean line of fire rather than taking a hurried shot.
  • Double check your target before squeezing the trigger, since mistakes are costly.

Strategy notes

Patience wins missions. Watch the scene for a while to learn how people move, then pick the moment when your target pauses in the open. Rushed shots tend to miss or hit someone else. Small details in the briefing, such as clothing or location, are often the only way to tell the right person from the crowd.

Final verdict

Tactical Assassin 2 is a tense, satisfying sniper game with a classic Flash feel. Its visuals are dated and missions can feel similar. Fans of patient shooting games will enjoy it.
