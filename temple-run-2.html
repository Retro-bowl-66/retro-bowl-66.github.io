Temple Run 2 Online

Inside Temple Run 2: Running from a monster through crumbling ruins

Temple Run 2, released by Imangi Studios in January 2013, polished the formula its predecessor made famous. You grab a cursed idol and flee through jungle ruins with a monstrous ape-like creature close behind. This browser version follows that idea, while the Temple Run name stays with Imangi.

Gameplay overview

You sprint forward automatically along cliffside paths, pressing keys to turn at corners, leap gaps and duck under obstacles. Coins line the route, and power-ups give short boosts such as shields or coin magnets. Some sections switch things up, sending you down zip lines or into rattling mine carts. A single stumble near the monster ends the run.

Beginner's checklist

  • Turn just as you reach a corner, not before.
  • Lean toward coins only when the path ahead is clear.
  • Watch for mine carts and zip lines, which change how you move.

Strategy notes

Anticipation beats reflex. Paths fork and drop away quickly, so keep your eyes on what is coming rather than on your runner. Do not chase every coin, because a missed turn costs far more than a few coins are worth. As speed builds, steady inputs work better than frantic ones. Power-ups are worth grabbing when they appear safely.

Final verdict

Temple Run 2 is a polished, exciting runner with satisfying variety. Its loop is repetitive over long sessions, and the browser version may lack some features. Fans of endless runners will find it a strong choice.
