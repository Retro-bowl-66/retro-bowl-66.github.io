Territorial Online

Inside Territorial: Painting the map in your color, border by border

Territorial is about as readable as strategy gets. David Tschacher's August 2020 release turns empire building into patches of color creeping over a map, each one a player or bot with ambitions. Your patch begins tiny, swells into unclaimed ground and sooner or later presses against someone else's, and then the real decisions start: grow, strike or wait.

Gameplay overview

Troops accumulate inside your borders over time, and spending them is how you grow: pushing into unclaimed land costs little, while invading a defended neighbor costs a lot. A bigger territory produces more troops, but a long, thin border is hard to protect. The aim is to become the dominant power on the map, swallowing or outlasting every rival.

Beginner's checklist

  • Claim neutral land first before picking fights with neighbors.
  • Keep a reserve of troops so you are never defenseless.
  • Attack rivals who are busy fighting someone else.

Strategy notes

Overreaching is the classic mistake. A sprawling border spreads your troops thin, while sitting on a huge reserve wastes what it could have won. Pounce on a neighbor whose army is tied up elsewhere, and avoid fighting on two fronts at once. Sometimes the strongest move is a pause that lets your numbers swell. Keep scanning the whole map for rising threats.

Final verdict

Territorial is a clean, tense strategy game with surprising depth. Matches can be decided early, and the visuals are very simple. Players who enjoy quick, readable strategy will find it compelling.
