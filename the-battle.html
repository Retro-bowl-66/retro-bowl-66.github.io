The Battle Online

Inside The Battle: Old-school Flash defense with careful spending

Tower defense practically defined a certain era of browser gaming, and Free World Group's The Battle is a no-nonsense example from those Flash days. A column of enemies trudges along a fixed road; you line that road with guns and hope the math works out. The formula holds no surprises, which is partly why it remains comfortable to play.

Gameplay overview

Before and during each wave, you spend gold on defensive structures along the route and decide which existing ones deserve improvements. Kills refill the coffers. Waves get longer and hardier, and every enemy that slips through to the end costs you, so the game is really a running budget problem dressed up as a battlefield.

Beginner's checklist

  • Build your first defenses at a bend where they can fire on two stretches.
  • Keep a little gold in reserve for emergencies in later waves.
  • Spread firepower along the route so no stretch is left unguarded.

Strategy notes

Gaps kill runs. A heavily fortified corner means little if the final stretch is empty, so make sure every section of the road is under fire before stacking upgrades. Then concentrate improvements where enemies bunch up. Watch which types slip through, and answer them specifically instead of buying more of what is already failing.

Final verdict

The Battle is a solid, old-fashioned tower defense game. It brings nothing new and looks dated today. Fans of classic Flash strategy will still find it a comfortable few rounds.
