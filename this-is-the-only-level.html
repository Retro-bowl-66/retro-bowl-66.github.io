This Is The Only Level Online

Inside This Is The Only Level: One room, endless variations

John Cooney, known online as jmtb02, built a famous Flash game in August 2009 around a delightful joke. There is exactly one level: a small room, a blue elephant, some spikes and an exit pipe. Finish it, and you are sent right back, except something has changed. The fun is in working out what.

Gameplay overview

Each stage keeps the same layout but alters a rule. Controls may be reversed, the screen may go dark, the elephant may respond only to certain inputs, or something else entirely may be different. The stage title is usually a cryptic clue about the twist. Reaching the pipe completes the stage, and the next variation begins immediately.

Beginner's checklist

  • Read each stage title closely, since it hints at the rule change.
  • Try obvious inputs first, then less obvious ones like the mouse.
  • Learn the room layout well, because it never changes.

Strategy notes

The room is the constant, so memorize it. Once you know where the spikes, platforms and pipe are, you can focus entirely on the new rule. Stage titles are often puns, so think laterally about what they might mean. When nothing seems to work, experiment with keys and clicks you have not tried, because some stages use surprising inputs.

Final verdict

This Is The Only Level is a witty, inventive classic that still holds up. Some stages are obscure enough to frustrate, and the visuals are basic. Puzzle fans who enjoy creative twists will love it.
