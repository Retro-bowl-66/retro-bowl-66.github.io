Tiny Islands Online

Inside Tiny Islands: Small puzzles on very small islands

David King's Tiny Islands, dated 2019, is a puzzle game that likes to keep things compact. Each challenge sits on a miniature island built in Unity WebGL, and solving it depends on noticing how its parts relate to each other. There is no timer and no pressure, which suits the game's quiet, observational style.

Gameplay overview

Each island arrives as a self-contained puzzle, a handful of pieces arranged in a way that is not yet right. You turn it over in your mind, test an idea and watch what changes. The answers tend to click once you spot a single overlooked detail. Solve one island and the next appears, usually with a new idea to absorb.

Beginner's checklist

  • Look at every part of an island before making your first move.
  • Test one idea at a time so you can see what each change does.
  • Step away briefly if stuck, since fresh eyes often spot the answer.

Strategy notes

Observation comes before action. Many puzzles hinge on one relationship between pieces, so look for symmetry, alignment or patterns that suggest a solution. Small experiments are cheap, so try ideas freely and undo them if they fail. When the answer seems impossible, it usually involves a piece you dismissed as irrelevant.

Final verdict

Tiny Islands is a calm, pleasant puzzle game with charming presentation. It is short and some puzzles may feel too easy. Players who enjoy relaxed, thoughtful puzzles will like it.

Details
