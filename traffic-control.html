Traffic Control Online

Inside Traffic Control: Keeping a busy junction crash-free

Traffic engineers spend careers on problems like this, and InLogic Games squeezes one into a quick timing game. Traffic Control, released in April 2023, puts you in charge of a single crossroads where cars arrive from every direction. Your job is deciding who goes and who waits, without letting anyone collide.

Gameplay overview

Cars roll up to the junction, and a click on any of them toggles between halting and proceeding. Let too many through at once and they smash into each other; hold them too long and the queue backs up. The flow quickens over time, so juggling several streams becomes the real challenge. The goal is to keep things moving safely for as long as possible.

Beginner's checklist

  • Watch the busiest direction and manage it first.
  • Halt cars early rather than at the last possible moment.
  • Release waiting cars in small batches to avoid jams.

Strategy notes

Think in gaps. A car only needs a brief window to cross safely, so look for openings and release vehicles into them. Holding one direction for too long creates backups that become hard to clear. As speed increases, prioritize avoiding crashes over keeping every car moving, since a short wait never ends a game.

Final verdict

Traffic Control is a tense, satisfying timing game with a clean premise. It lacks variety and can feel repetitive. Players who enjoy multitasking under pressure will like it.
