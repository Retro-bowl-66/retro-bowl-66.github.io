Trimps Online

Inside Trimps: A tiny colony that grows into something enormous

Trimps, from Greensatellite and released in May 2015, begins with almost nothing: a button to gather resources and a handful of small creatures. Over many hours, it grows into one of the deepest incremental games around, with jobs, buildings, combat, maps and a prestige system that resets your progress in exchange for long-term power.

Gameplay overview

Early on you click to gather the three basic materials, food, wood and metal, and put trimps to work collecting them for you. Buildings house more of them, and research unlocks upgrades. Soon, trimps fight through zones of enemies, earning resources and equipment. A portal mechanic lets you restart with permanent bonuses, and each run pushes further than the last.

Beginner's checklist

  • Assign trimps to jobs as soon as possible to automate gathering.
  • Build housing steadily to keep your population growing.
  • Read the portal mechanic carefully before your first reset.

Strategy notes

Trimps rewards patience and planning. Balance jobs so no resource becomes a bottleneck, and keep building housing to grow your workforce. In combat, upgrade equipment before pushing into harder zones. Portal resets feel painful at first, but each one makes you stronger, so learn when to restart rather than grinding indefinitely.

Final verdict

Trimps is a remarkably deep, rewarding incremental game for patient players. Its slow start and dense systems will deter some. Fans of long-term idle games will find it excellent.
