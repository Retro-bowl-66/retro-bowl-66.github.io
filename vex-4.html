Vex 4 Online

Inside Vex 4: A four-year wait and a tougher set of acts

Four years after Vex 3, Amazing Adam returned in October 2018 with a fourth entry that refines rather than reinvents. The stickman moves the same way, but the acts are longer and the hazard combinations nastier. Players who conquered the previous game will find plenty here to test them.

Gameplay overview

Fresh traps and obstacle combinations fill each act. Flags along the route act as restart points, so a failure only costs the most recent stretch, and coins sit in tempting spots for players who like extra risk. The goal is to finish each act and unlock the next, with later acts demanding near-perfect execution from start to finish.

Beginner's checklist

  • Revisit Vex 3 briefly if your timing feels rusty.
  • Grab coins only when the route to them looks safe.
  • Master each section between checkpoints before rushing ahead.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – slide / Dodge
  • Left / Right Arrows – move Left / Right
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Longer acts reward stamina. Rather than racing through, treat each checkpoint as a mini-level and master it before moving on. New hazards often combine familiar elements in unexpected ways, so watch carefully on your first attempt. Coins are tempting but risky, so skip them when a mistake would cost a big chunk of progress.

Final verdict

Vex 4 is a polished, demanding sequel that will satisfy series fans. It changes little, and the difficulty can be punishing. Players who enjoyed earlier Vex games should play it.

Details

  • Category: Platformer Games
  • Launched: October 2018
  • Price: Free browser version
  • Install: None, plays in a tab
