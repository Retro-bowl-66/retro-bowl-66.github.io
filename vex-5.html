Inside Vex 5: More acts, more saws and finer margins

Amazing Adam's November 2020 entry continues the series' steady climb in difficulty. Vex 5 adds new acts and obstacles that test the stickman's full move set, and by now the series knows exactly what it is: a tough, fair platformer built on memorization, timing and sheer stubbornness.

Gameplay overview

Each act is a long run of hazards punctuated by restart flags, with coins scattered in awkward spots. You hop, slide, wall jump and dodge to get through, and new obstacle types keep the patterns fresh. Dying sends you back to the last flag, so progress comes in bursts as each section finally clicks.

Beginner's checklist

  • Approach each new obstacle cautiously the first time you meet it.
  • Use checkpoints as practice zones rather than racing past them.
  • Take breaks when frustration starts affecting your timing.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – slide / Dodge
  • Left / Right Arrows – move Left / Right
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Patience is the secret. Vex 5 asks for precise sequences, so learn each section's rhythm before trying to run it smoothly. When a hazard seems unfair, watch it a few more times, because there is almost always a safe window. Focus on clean execution rather than speed, since one mistake costs more time than careful play.

Final verdict

Vex 5 is a confident, challenging entry with inventive new hazards. It is very similar to its predecessors and can frustrate. Fans of tough platformers will enjoy it.
