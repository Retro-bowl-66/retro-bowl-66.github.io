Vex 6 Online

Inside Vex 6: The latest Vex and the sharpest tests yet

Less than a year after Vex 5, Amazing Adam released Vex 6 in October 2021, bringing new obstacles to a series that had already become a fixture of browser platforming. The stickman's moves remain familiar, but the acts lean harder on combinations that demand quick, accurate inputs.

Gameplay overview

Every act is a chain of traps, shifting platforms and timed blades, and the stickman's usual repertoire of hops, slides and dodges is all you have to get past them. Restart flags break the acts into chunks, and coins tucked into dangerous corners tempt the brave. Hazards combine in more devious ways than before, so each section plays like a small puzzle as well as a reflex test.

Beginner's checklist

  • Expect tougher combinations than in earlier games from the very first act.
  • Study each new section before attempting it at speed.
  • Leave the riskiest coins for a second playthrough.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – slide / Dodge
  • Left / Right Arrows – move Left / Right
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Think of each section as a puzzle. Before moving, identify every hazard, its timing and the safe path through, then execute calmly. Rushing leads to repeated deaths, while careful observation often reveals an easier route. If you get stuck, step away for a moment; fresh eyes often spot the solution.

Final verdict

Vex 6 is a tough, well-designed platformer that keeps the series in good shape. Its difficulty and familiarity may not suit everyone. Fans of Vex and precision platforming will enjoy the challenge.

Details

  • Studio: Amazing Adam
  • Category: Platformer Games
  • Launched: October 2021
  • Price: Free browser version
  • Install: None, plays in a tab
