Inside Vex 8: The newest Vex, and a fair test of the series

Vex 8, released by Amazing Adam in October 2023, is the most recent chapter of the stickman parkour series, and it carries the polish of a franchise that has been honing one idea for close to a decade. Fresh obstacles join the familiar ones, but the appeal is unchanged: tight controls, harsh traps and generous restart points.

Gameplay overview

Your stickman works through acts made of spikes, blades, moving platforms and narrow ledges, combining hops, slides and dodges to stay alive. Flags mark your progress through each act, and coins reward players willing to stray from the safest route. New hazard types demand that you rethink habits formed in earlier games. Completing every act is the ultimate aim.

Beginner's checklist

  • Newcomers can start here, since the basics take only minutes to learn.
  • Spend a few attempts on each new hazard before trying to rush past it.
  • Use restart flags as natural pause points to plan the next section.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – slide / Dodge
  • Left / Right Arrows – move Left / Right
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Strategy notes

Habits from earlier Vex games help, but new traps sometimes punish them, so watch before assuming. Keep your movements deliberate, because many deaths come from holding a direction a fraction too long. Take a break after repeated failures, since fatigue makes timing worse. Once you finish, replaying acts for coins is a good way to sharpen precision.

Final verdict

Vex 8 is a polished, demanding platformer and a fair showcase of what the series does well. It offers little that is radically new. Both fans and curious newcomers will find a solid challenge.
