Volley Random Online

Inside Volley Random: Floppy volleyball with a one-button brain

RHM Interactive clearly enjoys putting ragdoll athletes into sports, and Volley Random, from February 2021, applies the treatment to volleyball. Your players wobble across the court, the ball is absurdly large, and the only thing you control is when they leap. You can face the computer or hand the other key to a friend.

Gameplay overview

Pressing your jump key launches your floppy players into the air, where they swat at the oversized ball and hopefully send it back over the net. If it lands on your side, the point goes to the opponent. Like other games in this family, conditions may shift from round to round, so the ball or court can behave differently next time. First to the target score wins.

Beginner's checklist

  • Jump as the ball drops toward your players, not while it is still high.
  • Watch which way your players are facing before you press the key.
  • Give each new round a moment to see how the conditions have changed.

Control scheme

  • Up Arrow / W Key – jump

Strategy notes

Timing is the only real lever, so use it carefully. Jumping too early sends your players flopping uselessly under the ball; too late and it hits the floor. Aim to meet it at the top of their leap. Because your players face a direction, the angle of contact matters more than raw power. Against a friend, the laughs outweigh any deep strategy, and that is fine.

Final verdict

Volley Random is a silly, chaotic sports game that shines with two players. Solo matches against the computer get repetitive. Perfect for quick laughs with a friend.
