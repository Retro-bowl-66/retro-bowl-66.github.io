Inside Wheely 3: The little red car's third round of puzzles

Wheely 3 continues the adventures of Mana Potion Studios' cheerful red car with new scenes and challenges. The formula is familiar from earlier chapters: help Wheely through each level by manipulating the world around him. Logic carries you further than luck, since most scenes are small machines waiting to be switched on in the right order.

Gameplay overview

Each scene presents a route blocked by obstacles, and you clear it by poking at objects, putting items to use and timing your actions. Some puzzles involve a chain of mechanisms, where one action enables another. Wheely needs a clear path to reach the exit, and figuring out the right sequence is the core challenge. Levels are short and self-contained.

Beginner's checklist

  • Trace the route Wheely needs to take before clicking anything.
  • Look for chains of mechanisms that trigger one another.
  • Experiment freely, since mistakes usually just mean a quick restart.

Strategy notes

Logic is your best tool. Ask what each object does and how it might affect Wheely's path. If one action seems to do nothing, it may be enabling something elsewhere. Work through possibilities methodically rather than clicking at random. The solutions are usually tidy once you see them.

Final verdict

Wheely 3 is a pleasant, logical puzzle game that continues the series nicely. It is brief and not particularly challenging. Fans of gentle point and click puzzles will enjoy it.
