Woodventure Online

Inside Woodventure: Gentle woodland puzzles with no hurry at all

Tomas Rychnovsky set his HTML5 puzzle adventure among trees and clearings, and the setting sets the tone: unhurried, quiet and a little whimsical. Woodventure hands you scenes to study and small problems to untangle, usually by spotting something easy to miss and putting two things together. It suits a slow afternoon better than a frantic one.

Gameplay overview

Every clearing is a small puzzle box. You look around, pocket whatever can be carried and try it on whatever seems to need it, occasionally pairing two items to make something new. Answers hinge on details hidden in the scenery. Nothing hurries you along, so wandering back and forth between spots is part of the rhythm.

Beginner's checklist

  • Survey the whole clearing before you try any item on anything.
  • Pair up items in your bag when a single item will not do the job.
  • Move slowly, since careful looking pays better than rapid clicking.

Strategy notes

Most progress comes from noticing. Run your eyes over each scene for anything that looks movable or out of place, and ask how it might connect to what you are carrying. When stuck, try combinations you have not attempted yet. Because the game never pushes you, there is no reason to rush, so treat it as a quiet brainteaser rather than a test.

Final verdict

Woodventure is a soothing, pleasant puzzle adventure with a charming setting. It is brief and its puzzles are mild. Fans of relaxed point and click games will like it.
