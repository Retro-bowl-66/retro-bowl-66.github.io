You Are Jeff Bezos Online

Inside You Are Jeff Bezos: Spending a vast fortune in a text satire

Kris Lorischild's October 2018 text game drops an almost incomprehensible fortune into your lap and asks what you plan to do with it. Every purchase and donation is subtracted from a balance so huge that even grand gestures barely register. That gap between enormous spending and a stubbornly enormous total is the whole joke, and also the whole argument. It is pure parody, made without the real executive's involvement.

Gameplay overview

Choices appear as clickable options: fund a cause, buy something absurd, tackle a social problem. Each comes with a short write-up of the effect, and the balance ticks down, slowly. As the options pile up, the game turns into a quiet commentary on wealth and priorities. There is no real win condition beyond seeing how far the money stretches.

Beginner's checklist

  • Fund a few huge causes early to see how little the balance moves.
  • Read each write-up, since that is where most of the jokes live.
  • Keep clicking to the end, where the point lands hardest.

Strategy notes

There is nothing to optimize, only things to notice. Try wildly different kinds of spending and watch how the numbers react, or fail to. The satire works through scale, so the more you spend, the sharper it gets. It makes a good prompt for a conversation about economics and inequality.

Final verdict

You Are Jeff Bezos is a sharp, funny bit of interactive satire. It is brief and offers little reason to replay. Anyone interested in economics or political humor will find it thought-provoking.
